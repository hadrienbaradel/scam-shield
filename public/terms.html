Scam Shield

Terms

Hackathon build, last updated October 26, 2026.

Scam Shield is a prototype built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon and offered free to invited testers.

  • A helper, not a guarantee. Scam Shield can miss a scam or flag a genuine message. It never claims a message is guaranteed safe. If money is involved, call the number on your card or statement — never a number from a message.
  • Reversible by design. Scam Shield only labels and moves messages; it never deletes them, and "Put it back" undoes any move.
  • Your trusted person gives an opinion as a favour; the decision stays yours.
  • You can stop at any time from Settings → "Delete my account".

See also the privacy page.
