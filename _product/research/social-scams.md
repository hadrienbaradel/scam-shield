# Social and job-offer scams: fake recruiters, hiring files, pig butchering, romance photos

Vlad's question (2026-10-29): strangers on LinkedIn send fake hiring files; strangers on social networks send photos and run pig-butchering scams; how can Prudence protect against this, for seniors and for everyone? Findings as of 2026-10-29. Spec draft: `specs/social-scams.md` (Idea). Day figures assume this two-person team reusing `/api/check/*`, `runCheck` (`src/sync/checker.js`) and `lib/muse.js`.

## Decision
- For Nov 4, in this order: (1) **the whole-conversation check** (paste a chat, add 1-3 screenshots, or forward several WhatsApp messages at once): the card names the stage of the scam and what comes next, 1.5 days, the video scene; (2) **the hiring-file check** (forward a file on WhatsApp or choose it in Check something: read in memory, its fingerprint asked to VirusTotal, never opened, never uploaded), 1.5 days, the "not only seniors" beat; (3) **"Who is this?"** on the same screenshot path (the profile plus the platform's own "About this account" page, and two taps: did they write first, have you met), +1 day. "Before you send money" (0.5 day) rides on the trusted-person loop.
- Why: the money is in the arc, not in one message. Crypto investment fraud was Americans' largest loss in 2025 ($7.2B, IC3) and its first messages ask for nothing. Meta says scammers "cycle people through many different platforms to ensure that any one service has only a limited view into the entire scam". Only the person sees the whole path; Prudence sits on their side of it, with their life and their family.
- Biggest gap today: Prudence judges one message at a time. The detector's only arc rule (affection, then money) needs two or more earlier messages, which only Gmail threads and demo cases supply; `/api/check/text`, `/api/check/image` and the WhatsApp channel pass an empty thread history. `IMAGE_PROMPT` copies ONE message, so a profile screenshot will likely come back "We could not find a message in this picture" (untested). No file is ever inspected: Gmail attachments are judged by name, WhatsApp documents get "I can only check texts, pictures and voice notes for now."

## The scams in 2025-2026
### (a) Fake recruiters and "hiring files" (LinkedIn first, then Telegram, WhatsApp, GitHub, a terminal)
- North Korea's Contagious Interview (UNC5342 at Google): fake recruiters on LinkedIn and job boards for crypto and tech firms, fake companies (BlockNovas LLC, Angeloper Agency, SoftGlideLLC), the talk moved to Telegram or Discord, then a coding test or a project to review from GitHub or npm; in video interviews a fake error asks for a "fix". Since Feb 2025 its JADESNOW downloader fetches the next stage from smart contracts on BNB Smart Chain and Ethereum ("EtherHiding"); BEAVERTAIL steals wallets and passwords, INVISIBLEFERRET is a backdoor (Google GTIG, 2025-10-16).
- npm: 338+ malicious packages and 50,000+ downloads from Jul 14 to Oct 10, 2025; contact mostly on LinkedIn, a take-home repo, deadline pressure to run npm install; code runs at `postinstall`, at import or on first run, its ciphertext hidden in a `LICENSE` file; typosquats of express, dotenv, body-parser, ethers.js, web3.js, truffle, hardhat (Socket, 2025-10-10). A later wave added at least 197 more (unverified).
- VS Code: repositories opened "under the pretext of a recruitment process or technical assignment" run `.vscode/tasks.json` when the folder opens (`"runOn": "folderOpen"`), piping a per-OS script into a shell once the folder is trusted (DevClass citing Jamf, 2026-01-22; GitLab ties it to Contagious Interview, 2026-05-04).
- ClickFake Interview (Sekoia, since Feb 2025): business developers and marketing managers at crypto firms record a video answer on a fake interview site; "a driver issue is preventing camera access" and the fix is a `curl` command pasted in CMD or Terminal that installs the GolangGhost backdoor; 184 invitations, 14 brands used as lures (Coinbase, Kraken, Robinhood, Tether...) (BleepingComputer, 2025-03-31). Operation DreamJob: fake offers to three European defence firms since March 2025 with trojanized MuPDF and TightVNC (ESET, 2025-10-23) (unverified).
- Everyday job scams (Microsoft Cyber Signals 9, 2025-04-16): offers that "promise high pay for minimal qualifications", "requests for payment" for training or background checks, "text messages, WhatsApp, nonbusiness Gmail accounts", interviews "with lip-syncing delays". Task scams (like videos or rate products in sets of 40, deposit to unlock the next set, crypto, first contact by text or WhatsApp): about 5,000 reports in 2023, about 20,000 in H1 2024; job-scam losses $223M in H1 2024 (FTC, 2024-12-12). IC3 2025: employment 24,688 complaints, $362.9M (60+: 2,853 and $78.7M); AI-involved employment scams about $13M. FTC: "One in three people who reported losing money to a job or business opportunity scam in 2025 said it started on social media" (2026-04-27).
- LinkedIn's defences: 99.7% of fake accounts stopped before a member report, 97.8% by automated defences (Jul-Dec 2025); over 100M members verified (2025-12-09; every member, job and Page verified is a goal, not a fact); "About this profile" shows when a profile was created and updated and whether a phone or work email is verified; uploads are screened for AI-generated photos; "We may warn you about messages that ask you to take the conversation to another platform" (2022-10-25). Limits: a badge proves who, not why (a verified or hijacked account still sends the repo); the warning lives on LinkedIn while the attack runs in Telegram, GitHub and a terminal; LinkedIn's API gives no one a member's messages, and another member's profile only to approved Sales Navigator partners.

### (b) Pig butchering (sha zhu pan)
- The arc (IC3 2025): contact "through text messages, social media sites, advertisements, or dating applications and then quickly move the conversation to a messaging platform", an investment group of "industry insiders" (crypto or gold), a fake platform or app showing fake profits and offering loans, "taxes and fees" at withdrawal, then recovery scams. Crypto investment fraud: $7.2B in 2025, the largest loss; investment clubs on social media and messaging apps about 1,600 complaints, $160M. People 60+: investment $3.52B; crypto-related $4.35B (42,271 complaints).
- Operation Level Up (FBI): 3,780 victims notified in 2025, 78% did not know they were being scammed, $225.9M estimated savings, 38 referred for suicide intervention; 8,000+ notified since Jan 2024. Prudence aims at the same warning, before the first deposit.
- Scale: at least $10B lost by Americans in 2024 to Southeast Asia-based scam operations, +66% (Treasury, 2025-09-08); crypto scams took at least $14B on chain in 2025, likely over $17B, impersonation +1,400%, operations buying from AI vendors 4.5 times more profitable (Chainalysis, 2026-01-13); hundreds of industrial-scale centres earning close to $40B a year, spreading to Africa, South Asia and the Pacific (UNODC, 2025-04) (unverified); staffed by trafficked forced labour (IC3, Meta).
- Where it starts: $2.1B lost in 2025 to scams that began on social media (8 times 2020), $1.1B of it investment; Facebook first, "WhatsApp and Instagram were a distant second and third" (FTC, 2026-04-27); "wrong number" texts are in the FTC's top five text scams of 2024 (`research/scam-market-stats.md`). Fake trading apps passed Apple and Google review in 2024 as calculators and news readers that opened only with an invitation code (Group-IB, 2024-10) (unverified).

### (c) Romance and the "photos" stage
- IC3 2025: confidence/romance $929.3M (60+: $584.0M, 10,188 complaints), about $19M with an AI nexus. FTC: "nearly 60% of people who reported losing money to a romance scam in 2025 said it started on a social media platform" ($298M there); some pivot to "a fake investment platform", others to sextortion.
- Photos are stolen or generated: 1,000+ LinkedIn profiles with AI faces found in 2022 (Stanford Internet Observatory via The Register) (unverified); people judge GAN faces at chance and rate them more trustworthy (Nightingale and Farid, PNAS 2022) (unverified); a Hong Kong ring used AI photos, then face-swap video calls, and took about US$46M (2024-10) (unverified). Rule for Prudence: never call a photo fake from its look; "the same photo is on other sites under another name" is a fact; "he will not video call" and "he talks about money" are the strong signals.

### (d) What Meta does, and where Prudence sits
- Takedowns: nearly 12M scam-centre accounts on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp in H1 2025 (2025-12-03), including 6.8M+ WhatsApp accounts banned before use (2025-08-05); 10.9M Facebook and Instagram scam-centre accounts (period not stated) and 159M+ scam ads removed in 2025, 92% before anyone reported them (2026-03-11); 1.4M+ accounts, Pages and groups and 63 arrests with the DOJ Scam Center Strike Force, the FBI, the Royal Thai Police, Microsoft, Coinbase and Starlink (2026-06-03); a National Elder Fraud Coordination Center member (2025-10-21); data sharing with 50+ financial institutions (FIRE) and the Global Signal Exchange (2025-12-03).
- In the apps: a WhatsApp safety overview when a stranger adds you to a group (2025-08), a warning before screen-sharing with an unknown contact on a video call (2025-10), device-linking warnings (2026-03); Facebook friend-request warnings on accounts with few mutual friends or another country (testing, 2026-03); Messenger scam detection on chats with someone new, with an opt-in AI review of recent messages, triggered for example by suspicious job offers (testing 2025-10, expanding 2026-03); facial recognition against celebrity-bait ads (2025-12).
- Position: Meta's layer is platform-wide (accounts, ads, chats with new contacts) and tuned for scale; Prudence is the personal layer that sees the path across apps because the person shows it, knows their life (their bank, whether they applied for a job, their trusted people), brings in the family, and sends them back to Meta's own Report and Block. It runs on Muse Spark and the WhatsApp Cloud API and replaces nothing Meta does.

## Demo scene (15-20 s)
- Linda's phone at phone width, WhatsApp open on "Daniel", who wrote to her "by mistake" on Instagram three weeks ago: "My uncle's app made me $8,400 this month. Start with $500, I will guide you." (0-4 s). She selects his last messages, taps Forward, picks Prudence (4-8 s). The reply lands under them: "Scam. This is the 'new friend, then investment' trick: he wrote to you by mistake, moved you to WhatsApp and now shows you his profits. Next he will ask you to put money on his app, then for a fee to take it out. Do not send anything. Show this to Thomas." (8-16 s).
- Narrator (16-20 s): "Meta's Muse Spark reads the whole conversation, not one message, and sees where it is going before the money moves." Optional 5 s cutaway for "not only seniors": a developer forwards `Assignment.pdf.exe` (the harmless EICAR test file) and gets "Do not open this file. It is a program, not a PDF, and dozens of security engines know it." (whether WhatsApp carries an .exe is unverified).

## Privacy stance
- Nothing is scraped: Prudence never opens LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook or dating-app pages; the person shows what they see (paste, screenshot, forward); the extension, later, captures the visible tab only on a click.
- Screenshots, photos and files are read in memory and never stored; only the verdict is kept, as `/api/check/image` does today.
- A file is never opened, run, unpacked to disk or uploaded: magic bytes, the names inside an archive and a SHA-256 fingerprint; VirusTotal gets the fingerprint only, because uploaded contents may reach its premium customers.
- No face recognition: a photo search runs only on a tap, sends that one picture, and reports where the same picture appears, never who the face is.
- The verdict is about this person's life: the answers they give ("never met"), what their own mail shows ("no application to this company in the emails we read"), their trusted people. A stranger's name and photo are someone else's data: used for the check, not kept.
