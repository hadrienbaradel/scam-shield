- **Pick:** Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 Wayfarer, matte black, clear lenses. It is the current model, has the best microphones and is in stock, and Meta has confirmed DAT support for it on the repo. A senior at a kitchen table should wear clear lenses; take Transitions (€529) only if the clear pair is gone. **Fallback:** Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 Wayfarer, matte black, clear lenses (€419; $379 unverified). It runs the same companion code and is named in the docs.
- **Code note:** Gen 3 shows up as `RAYBAN_META_OPTICS`, so never filter devices by type (Meta, GitHub #316).
- **Display:** a red "Scam" verdict on the HUD, recorded with the Meta AI app's "Record display", is a shot no other pair can give. The Display can be bought now: online in the US since Sep 23 with the store demo optional, and in France since Oct 13 online and at select Paris stores, with prescription lenses through EssilorLuxottica opticians (Meta). It still loses on the cost and the risks:
  - $799 / €899, and a Neural Band size to pick.
  - Unknown stock: Meta's variant pages showed out of stock in search snippets (unverified).
  - A HUD layout to build.
  - Open DAT bugs where the Display session ends as soon as the HFP microphone opens (GitHub Android #210, iOS #342).
- **Firmware, on every model:** a new pair installs the current glasses software at first pairing. On that software the DAT camera session ends when the wearer-focused HFP microphone connects. Meta says the fix will come in a glasses update, not in the SDK (GitHub #206, Oct 13-16). Shoot the letter first and listen afterwards, or use the in-stream camera audio, which is not beamformed and so picks up the other speaker better.

**Recommendation.** Vlad buys option A today, because the deadline sits with him: the full loop (photo from the glasses, verdict in the ear) has to run by Friday Oct 30, and the hard cut is his Nov 1 rehearsal.
1. Open Boulanger ref 1246866 and choose Haussmann Maison, Montparnasse or BHV for the 30-minute pickup, or call 09 69 32 32 23.

3. Tonight, pair the glasses in the Meta AI app (V292 or later; glasses v128 or later) and enable Developer Mode. On Friday, run the CameraAccess sample.

A second pair in Boston pays off only if two things hold: Linda must wear the glasses on camera in Hadrien's footage, and Hadrien has a phone that runs the companion. In that case he buys option C or D on Saturday Oct 31, once Vlad's loop has run end to end, and films on Nov 1 or Nov 2. If the scene is dropped, each pair can go back within its window (Boulanger and Best Buy 15 days, Meta.com 30 days from delivery). Keep the box and every accessory, and unpair and factory-reset the glasses first.
