# iPhone hooks: Shortcuts, extensions and what Apple already screens

What Scam Shield can hook on an iPhone with and without a native app, what each hook sees and shows, the setup a relative does, Apple's constraints, effort and whether it fits before Nov 4; written 2026-10-28 for iOS 17, 18 and 26 (26 is current). Day figures are the researcher's estimates for this team: no iOS code in the repo; the server offers `/api/check/text` and `/api/check/image` behind the session cookie and `/api/devices/check` (JSON `{from, body}`, bearer device token, 30 requests/min, no image variant).

## Decision

For Nov 4: the Shortcuts-only path, about 2 days in all: a screenshot shortcut on Back Tap, the Action Button or the Lock Screen (0.5-1 day including `POST /api/devices/check/image`), the Message automation that posts each incoming text to `/api/devices/check` and speaks the verdict (0.5-1 day), and the same shortcut enabled in the share sheet (0.25 day). No developer account, no review, nothing to install beyond an iCloud link, and the verdict lands as a notification, a spoken sentence and the existing web card. Do not build a native app this week: a share extension adds polish and no data (2-3 days), the Message Filter only files a text into a folder that iOS 26 already fills silently (2-3 days), Call Directory needs a number list we lack (1-2 days), Live Caller ID Lookup needs Apple's validation and a PIR stack (weeks), and CallKit never gives call audio. After the demo, real seniors keep the same shortcuts (a relative sets them up in about 10 taps); a native app waits for a paid Apple Developer membership (TestFlight internal testers need no review) and would carry the Report Junk extension, the one native hook with our own screen inside Messages and Phone. The video line is "iPhone sorts, Scam Shield explains": iOS 26 screens callers and spam but never says why, never knows the senior's life and never reaches email or WhatsApp.

## Build sheet

Shortcuts, rules shared by every shortcut
- Sharing: an iCloud link needs no setting ("Allow Untrusted Shortcuts" disappeared in iOS 15; "Private Sharing" in Settings > Shortcuts, off by default, concerns files received from contacts); shortcuts exported for anyone are signed on Apple's servers, the link preview no longer lists every action, and iCloud links can break, so keep the `.shortcut` file. "Allow Running Scripts" (Settings > Shortcuts > Advanced) is only for JavaScript on web pages; "Allow Sharing Large Amounts of Data" is a separate switch. Apple's guide: some automations run without confirmation, "the automation will not notify you when it's triggered", "you also may need to set individual actions to run automatically", and "Allow Running When Locked" sits under Edit > Info > Privacy.
- Prompts: each data access asks Allow Once, Always Allow or Don't Allow; revocable per shortcut (Privacy tab) and by Reset Privacy; the richer prompt previews the content and appears on undocumented rules. An unanswered prompt stalls the automation silently, sometimes missed under Do Not Disturb, and "Get Contents of URL" hanging inside a personal automation on iOS 18 is reported (unverified).
- Network: "Get Contents of URL" does GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE, custom headers (`Authorization: Bearer <device token>`) and a body of type JSON (top-level object only), Form (a file field makes a multipart upload) or File (third-party references, unverified); read the reply with "Get Dictionary Value" (`verdict.headline`; `/api/devices/check` returns `{checkId, verdict, guardians}`). Outputs: "Show Notification" (title and body, tappable), "Speak Text" (mixed reports inside automations: a MacRumors user says it stopped after iOS 16, others report garbled audio from clock triggers (unverified); the notification is primary, speech the bonus), "Open URL" to the web app (`/`, the Home Screen web app when installed). The device token is asked once at import (Import Question) from the pairing flow.

Screenshot shortcut
- Actions: Take Screenshot > Get Contents of URL (POST, Form, file = the screenshot) > Get Dictionary Value > Speak Text + Show Notification + Open URL; speak "Checking..." first to cover 5-10 s of Muse at low effort (feasibility #12). Plan B after the system screenshot gesture: "Get Latest Screenshots" (Photos permission; action name unverified).
- Server, 1-2 hours: `POST /api/devices/check/image` behind `deviceAuth`, same body as `/api/check/image` (multipart PNG/JPEG/WebP, 8 MB cap, `extractFromImage` then `runCheck`, 10/min); screenshots are 1-3 MB; crop or ignore the "shortcut running" banner at the top (about 120 px), which two community sources say is captured (2021, 2023; unverified on iOS 26).
- Launch points: Back Tap (iOS 14+, iPhone 8+): Settings > Accessibility > Touch > Back Tap > Double Tap or Triple Tap > the shortcut; two slots, about 30 options each, mistimed taps fail (unverified). Action Button (iPhone 15 Pro or later): Settings > Action Button > Shortcut > Choose a Shortcut. Control Center: Add a Control > Shortcut > Choose. Lock Screen (iOS 18+): press and hold > Customize > Lock Screen > replace Flashlight or Camera with a Shortcut control (third-party sources). Whether Shortcuts stays in the background when launched this way is not stated on Apple's pages.
- Relative's steps: 1) tap the iCloud link, Add Shortcut, paste the device token; 2) run it once, Always Allow for the API host; 3) Settings > Accessibility > Touch > Back Tap > Double Tap > Scam Shield (4 taps) or Settings > Action Button > Shortcut (3 taps). Fallback: a normal screenshot, then Share > Scam Shield from Photos.

Message automation
- Trigger: Shortcuts > Automation > + > Message, filters Sender and Message Contains; at least one field must be filled, "Any Sender" plus Message Contains " " is the catch-all (misses one-word texts, no regex), the catch-all works on sender or content but not both (unverified) > Next > Run Immediately. Since iOS 17 Message, Email, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are true automations; before 17 they could not run automatically. Apple requires a notification on every run ("Notify When Run" cannot be switched off under Run Immediately; "Run After Confirmation" is the default). The automation itself cannot be exported by iCloud link, only the shortcut it calls.
- Payload: Apple documents no input. A Feb 2026 post lists Sender, Message Content, Recipients and Name on the Message type (unverified); the STOP automation uses "Get phone numbers from shortcut input" then Find Contacts where Phone Number is it (unverified). Fires for iMessage and SMS, for any sender, with the screen off (unverified); lock-screen runs have mixed reports (an alarm automation waited for unlock, 55 "Me too"). One 2024 Apple Community reply claims the Sender must be a contact, contradicted by the "Any Sender" option (unverified).
- Shortcut: Get phone numbers from input > Find Contacts > if none, Get Contents of URL (POST JSON `{from, body}` to `/api/devices/check`) > Show Notification + Speak Text + Open URL; add a `speak` sentence (EN/FR) to the response if `headline` reads badly aloud. The Contacts filter is the privacy promise; without it every family text reaches the server.
- Relative's steps: 1) iCloud link, Add Shortcut, token; 2) run once, Always Allow for Contacts and the host; 3) Automation > Message > Any Sender, Message Contains " " > Next > Run Immediately > pick the shortcut (8-10 taps). Fallback: the screenshot shortcut.

Email automation
- Trigger Email, Apple Mail only: filters Sender, Subject Contains, Account, Recipient, all must match; runs without confirmation since iOS 17; a push-capable account is advised for near-real-time firing and a trigger on an iCloud alias never fired on iOS 18 (unverified); the input payload is undocumented. Not a Gmail hook: skip.

Share Sheet shortcut
- Apple's input types: Safari webpages (also from Safari View Controller in other apps), URLs (an Article with title, author, body, excerpt for a web URL), Images, Text, Rich text (HTML from web pages), Files, PDFs, Media; a shortcut accepting images also appears when sharing a Safari page; the shortcut shows only in apps sharing the enabled types. No email-message type: Mail hands over selected text or attachments, never the .eml with headers (a drag-into-draft workaround exists, unverified). Messages bubbles offer Forward, not Share (one iOS 18.5 report of the More option missing; unverified). WhatsApp: forward to up to five chats, export to the share sheet unverified. Voicemail: Phone > Voicemail > Share > a file (.m4a, unverified) when the carrier supports Visual Voicemail; a Media-input shortcut can post it, with a transcription step on the server (1 day, voicemail.md). Runs in the foreground with the Shortcuts sheet visible.

Native app with Share extension
- Receives `NSItemProvider` attachments (text, URLs, images, files) from `extensionContext.inputItems`; `NSExtensionActivationRule` restricts types (`NSExtensionActivationSupportsText`); for Safari, `NSExtensionJavaScriptPreprocessingFile` names a script whose `ExtensionPreprocessingJS.run(completionFunction)` returns a key-value object (results under `NSExtensionJavaScriptPreprocessingResultsKey`, `finalize()` may edit the page; needs `NSExtensionActivationSupportsWebPageWithMaxCount`); the archive guide dates from 2017. Calls the API with the device token, no Shortcuts prompts. 2-3 days; nothing Messages, Mail or WhatsApp withholds from the share sheet becomes available.

Message Filter extension
- Extension point `com.apple.identitylookup.message-filter`; `ILMessageFilterQueryRequest` carries `sender`, `messageBody`, `receiverISOCountryCode`; SMS and MMS from unknown senders only (Apple's iOS 26 page adds RCS), never contacts, never iMessage, never a sender replied to three times or more; the extension has no network of its own and cannot write to shared containers.
- Deferral: `deferQueryRequestToNetwork(completion:)`; the system POSTs JSON `{"_version": 1, "query": {"sender": "14085550001", "message": {"text": "This is a message"}}, "app": {"version": "1.1"}}` to the single `ILMessageFilterExtensionNetworkURL` in Info.plist (one URL honoured, unverified) and returns `ILNetworkResponse` (`urlResponse`, `data`). Answers: `none`, `allow`, `junk`, `promotion`, `transaction` (`filter` deprecated) plus iOS 16 sub-actions (transactional Others, Finance, Orders, Reminders, Health, Weather, Carrier, Rewards, PublicServices; promotional Others, Offers, Coupons) declared through `ILMessageFilterCapabilitiesQueryResponse`.
- Needs: Associated Domains entitlement with `messagefilter:<domain>`; `apple-app-site-association` served at `https://<domain>/.well-known/` with a valid certificate and no redirects, fetched through Apple's CDN within 24 hours, or `?mode=developer` to bypass the CDN while the tunnel serves it; development errors `networkURLUnauthorized` and "messagefilter error code 3" reported (unverified). Works in Xcode and TestFlight builds (nothing in Apple's docs limits it to the App Store; unverified).
- iOS 26 context: Filter Spam on by default, no notification, links and replies disabled until "Move to Inbox"; Screen Unknown Senders off by default (on if "Filter Unknown Senders" was on in iOS 18); Time Sensitive notifications on by default, per-type choices only in the US, Brazil and India; allowed messages stay visible for eight hours. Senior setup: Messages > Filters > Manage Filtering > Text Message Filter > Scam Shield. Whether a third-party `junk` suppresses the notification and which folder it lands in: unverified.

Report Junk extension
- Enabled one at a time in Settings > Phone > SMS/Call Reporting; the system shows `ILClassificationUIExtensionViewController` (our UI, system Cancel and Done) from "Report Messages" or Recents > Report; responses `none`, `reportNotJunk`, `reportJunk`, `reportJunkAndBlockSender`; the report goes to `ILClassificationExtensionNetworkReportDestination` (associated domain `classificationreport`) or by SMS (`ILClassificationExtensionSMSReportDestination`, 7726 or 33700); the container is deleted after each run; iOS 12+. 1-2 days inside the native app, for the Report feature (feasibility #18).

Call Directory, Live Caller ID, CallKit
- Call Directory: inside `beginRequest(with:)`, `addIdentificationEntry(withNextSequentialPhoneNumber:label:)` and `addBlockingEntry(withNextSequentialPhoneNumber:)` in ascending order; loaded only when the extension launches ("you can't make a request to a web service to find information about an incoming call"); checked after contacts; shows "(App Name) Caller ID: label"; reload through `CXCallDirectoryManager`; enable under Settings > Phone > Call Blocking & Identification (third-party guides). No number source today (feasibility #24).
- Live Caller ID Lookup (iOS 18+): relay access requested at icloud.developer.apple.com/dashboard/identity and Apple validates the endpoints; Info.plist `NSPIRConfiguration` with `PIRServerURL` and `PrivacyPassIssuerURL`; blocking is one byte, identity a `CallIdentity` protobuf (name, HEIC icon, `cache_expiry_minutes`, PERSON or BUSINESS); Apple's example server (Swift 6.1+) "should not be run in production"; forums: a request three weeks without reply, two apps pending review, the iOS 18 beta PIR service working about 1% of the time (unverified).
- CallKit: no API for the live audio of a call the app does not carry, "each CallKit app actually controls and manages its own audio stream" (Apple DTS, Aug 2026).

Home Screen web app
- Web Push (iOS 16.4+) only for a web app added to the Home Screen (manifest `display` standalone or fullscreen), requested from a user tap; Badging API supported. `getUserMedia` works in standalone web apps since iOS 13.4, and `<input type=file capture>` photographs a letter. Web Share Target: WebKit bug 194593 filed 2019-02-13, status NEW, nobody assigned, last activity 2026-05-23; Chrome and Firefox on iOS are WebKit too. Outbound `navigator.share` since Safari 12.2.

Apple's own screens on iOS 26
- Call Screening: Settings > Apps > Phone > Screen Unknown Callers: Never, Ask Reason for Calling (the caller answers before the phone rings), Silence (to voicemail); off while roaming and for 24 hours after an emergency call; standard rates may apply; Call Filtering lists Unknown Callers and Spam; FaceTime only silences. Press adds a live transcript, iPhone 11 and later, no Apple Intelligence needed; some regions lack "Ask Reason for Calling" (unverified).
- Live Voicemail (iOS 17+): the transcript appears on the call screen while the caller speaks; US and Canada English at launch; iOS 18 added more English regions, Spanish, French for France, German, Japanese, Mandarin, Cantonese and Brazilian Portuguese (unverified). Shortcuts cannot read these transcripts; the senior screenshots them.

Distribution
- Free Apple ID: Xcode installs on a cabled iPhone with a profile that expires after 7 days (Apple DTS, hedged "IIRC"); reported limit 10 App IDs per 7 days, no TestFlight, no ad hoc (unverified); the "3 apps" figure has no source. Extensions ship inside such a build.
- TestFlight (paid program): internal testers up to 100 App Store Connect users, no review; external up to 10,000 after a first-build review with no published duration (Runway average about 7.7 h in May 2026, forum waits of two weeks or more; unverified); builds expire after 90 days; public links; Managed Apple Accounts in reserved domains excluded. Developer Program enrollment: Apple quotes 24-48 h, early-2026 threads report 2-7+ weeks, one individual stuck 9 weeks (user reports). App Store: no SMS-filter-specific guideline found; out of scope.
