# Ask channels: send Scam Shield something suspicious, get the verdict back in the same channel

Answers, as of 2026-10-28: through which channels a senior or a relative can send something suspicious and get the verdict back where they sent it, what arrives, who can use each before verification, what the public needs, cost, days of work and whether it fits before Nov 4. Corrects `feasibility.md` #9, #10, #15, #16, #25. Effort figures assume this two-person Node 22 / Fastify team reusing `/api/check/text`, `/api/check/image` (`src/routes/mail.js`) and `lib/muse.js`.

## Decision

For the Nov 4 video: the WhatsApp Cloud API bot on the test number (a Meta product, a free `forwarded` flag, voice notes, ~2 days, needs the tunnel), then the `<Record>` phone hotline if a day remains (no app, no typing, Muse Voice Transcribe on stage, ~1.5 days); Telegram only as the off-camera harness for the shared inbound-check-reply path (half a day, no public URL); the fridge QR is a half-day add-on once a no-sign-in check page exists. SMS cannot answer in our own words on a Twilio trial, so it is not a Nov 4 scene; iMessage, Signal and RCS are out. For real seniors after the hackathon: France WhatsApp first (87% of messaging users; real number, display-name review, business verification), then Messenger, then the hotline (for the 30% of 70+ without a smartphone); US Messenger first (59% of 65+ are on Facebook; App Review plus Business Verification), then the hotline and a verified toll-free SMS/MMS number, WhatsApp third (20% of 65+).

## Build sheet

- **WhatsApp Cloud API**
  - Payload: `messages[]` with `from` (wa_id), `id`, `timestamp`, `type`; `text.body`; `image.{id,mime_type,sha256,caption,url}`; `audio.{id,mime_type,sha256,voice,url}` (`audio/ogg; codecs=opus`, `voice: true` for a WhatsApp recording); `document.{id,mime_type,sha256,filename,caption,url}`; `context.forwarded` (5 forwards or fewer) or `context.frequently_forwarded` (more than 5), present only when forwarded; `referral` only for Click-to-WhatsApp ads; unknown types arrive as `type: "unsupported"` with `messages.errors`.
  - Media: `url` in the webhook since Nov 12, 2025 (gradual rollout); otherwise `GET graph.facebook.com/<VERSION>/<MEDIA_ID>` returns a URL valid 5 minutes; ids last 7 days; download with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`; inbound cap 100 MB (error 131052); sending: audio AAC, AMR, MP3, MP4, OGG Opus mono at 16 MB, images JPEG or PNG 5 MB, documents 100 MB.
  - Webhook: public HTTPS with a valid certificate (self-signed refused); the `GET` verification echoes `hub.challenge` when `hub.mode=subscribe` and `hub.verify_token` match; each `POST` is signed `X-Hub-Signature-256: sha256=<HMAC of the raw body with the App Secret>`; answer 200; retries with decreasing frequency for 36 hours, so dedupe on `messages[].id`.
  - Window and price: a user message opens a 24-hour service window (reset by each message); free-form replies only inside it, templates after; per-message billing since July 1, 2025; service replies were free Nov 1, 2024 to Sep 30, 2026, then 1,000 free per business number per month from Oct 1, 2026, charged from the 1,001st, no rollover, stopped at the tier without a payment method; inbound never billed; in-window utility templates charged since Oct 1, 2026; governments and non-profits free through Dec 31, 2027; the free 7-day entry point covers Click-to-WhatsApp ads only, not wa.me links; the US sits under the North America rate, France is standalone; USD rates live only in the rate-card downloads (third parties cite India ₹0.145 and Brazil ~$0.0068, no US or FR figure (unverified)); Meta Business Agent replies cost $2.00 per 1M tokens since July 1, 2026 (launch June 3, 2026, third-party (unverified)).
  - Before verification: Get Started auto-generates a test number and sends `hello_world`; the temporary token expires quickly, so create a system user with a permanent token holding `business_management`, `whatsapp_business_messaging`, `whatsapp_business_management`; the allowlist takes 5 phones confirmed by code, error 131030 beyond (five is a third-party figure (unverified)); test-asset messages are not billed; a number registered to Cloud API cannot run the consumer app, and a number in consumer use must be deleted first.
  - Public: an owned number with country and area code (no short codes) that receives SMS or voice; display-name review (`PENDING_REVIEW`, `DECLINED`, `AVAILABLE_WITHOUT_REVIEW`, `EXPIRED`); a two-step PIN; 2 numbers per new portfolio, 20 after business verification or the 2,000 tier; messaging limit 250 unique users per rolling 24 h, then 2,000 (scaling path, e.g. business verification), 10,000, 100,000, unlimited; it counts only messages outside windows, so the ask-bot is never limited, proactive guardian templates are.
  - Groups and QR: a Business number cannot join a family group; API groups are `POST /<PHONE_NUMBER_ID>/groups`, business-created, no manual add, invite link sent by an approved template (approval up to 24 h), OBA required, about 8 participants (unverified), `group_service` priced per delivered message (third-party (unverified)); `POST /{phone-number-id}/message_qrdls` with `prefilled_message` (140 chars) and `generate_qr_image` SVG or PNG returns `https://wa.me/message/<CODE>`, 2,000 codes per number; the `wa.me/<number>?text=` form is widely documented but (unverified).
  - Calling API: user-initiated calls free, WebRTC Opus or SIP; needs the 2,000 tier except testers and test numbers; disabled by default on test numbers; business-initiated calls unavailable to the US, Canada, Egypt, Vietnam, Nigeria.
  - Build (~2 days): day 1 Fastify `GET` and `POST /webhooks/whatsapp`, dispatch on `type` to the `/api/check/text` logic, `extractFromImage`, `ffmpeg -i in.ogg -ac 1 -ar 16000 -f wav` then `POST https://api.meta.ai/v1/asr/transcribe`, Muse PDF input; reply with `POST /<PHONE_NUMBER_ID>/messages`; bypass `requireUser` by mapping `from` to a Scam Shield user or running an anonymous profile and saying so on the card; day 2 idempotency, QR, FR/EN copy, mock-LLM tests; make `forwarded` a detector fact.
  - Senior steps: 1) scan the magnet (saves the contact), 2) long-press the message, 3) Forward, 4) Scam Shield, 5) send (3 taps once saved); for a voice note hold the microphone and talk. Fallback: "I could not hear this; type the words", or send a screenshot.
