# Landline: what Scam Shield can plug into on a home phone in France and the US
Answers what a home landline offers Scam Shield in France (Livebox, Freebox, Bbox, SFR Box) and the US (Xfinity, Fios, AT&T, Spectrum, Vonage, Ooma), what a Scam Shield number on Twilio needs, how long each route takes and whether it fits before Nov 4; written 2026-10-28. Live listening law stays in `_product/research/feasibility.md` #10.

## Decision
For Nov 4: build the answering number (A) on a US Twilio local number today and order a French 09 number in parallel; ingest voicemail-to-e-mail through the existing Gmail sync (B), which covers Free, Bouygues, Fios, Xfinity and Ooma homes with no telephony code and is the fallback if the French number is late; put the operator checklist (C) in the relative's onboarding. After Nov 4, France stays on forwarding, because no French box documents simultaneous ring and Orange already ships a "say your name" screener that Orange homes should simply enable, while US VoIP homes get the simultaneous-ring screener (E) in the slot Nomorobo vacated on 1 January 2026. One scene, not the headline: 85% of French 70+ keep a fixed phone but a line carries 37 minutes a month, and 35% of US 65+ live with a landline, 10% landline-mostly or landline-only.

## Build sheet
A. Answering number (forward on no answer)
- Call flow: inbound webhook on the Twilio number; `<Say>` "Scam Shield for [first name]. This call is screened and may be recorded. Who is calling and what is it about?"; `<Gather input="speech" language="fr-FR">` (fr-FR on the `phone_call`, `experimental_conversations` and `experimental_utterances` models; Google STT V2 or Deepgram nova selectable, `language="multi"` with nova-3; `speechTimeout` integer or `auto`; result posted as `SpeechResult` with `Confidence`; `partialResultCallback` for partials; 60 s cap; `hints` up to 500 entries); `<Record>`; Muse at low effort with the senior's context; `<Say>` the safe message; SSE card and guardian e-mail. Timing: prompt about 4 s, caller speaks 5-10 s, end-pointing 1-2 s, Muse about 5 s: verdict 6-8 s after the caller stops. On timeout or error take a message, never hang up on a caller; if forwarding fails the carrier voicemail still answers.
- Twilio prices (page dated August 2026): US local number $1.15/month, inbound local $0.0085/min, toll-free $2.15/month and $0.022/min, outbound US/Canada $0.014/min; France local number $1.35/month, inbound $0.0100/min, outbound to French landlines $0.0187/min, to French mobiles $0.1603/min ($0.0404 from the EEA), SIP/BYOC $0.004/min; recording $0.0025/min, storage $0.0005/min/month, transcription $0.05/min; `<Gather>` speech price not retrieved. About $0.02-0.05 per screened call before Muse (about $0.009 per e-mail-size judgment, feasibility #20).
- French number: regulatory bundle, validated by a human in up to three days. Local 01-05 for an individual: government ID, passport or residence permit plus a proof of address in France (ID with address, utility bill, tax notice, rent receipt, title deed; no PO box); 09 national: the same documents, address in France; business: K-bis plus SIREN/SIRET; 0800-0805: businesses only; mobile numbers: one natural person, no automated calling; local and national numbers are listed for manual, not automated, outbound; an 01 number needs an Île-de-France address and goes through support; a 2025 recap says France dropped the ID requirement (unverified, conflicts with the guidelines page). Order on day one if the video shows a French handset.
- Trial limits: inbound only from at most five verified caller IDs, a trial announcement before any TwiML, calls cut at 10 minutes, concurrency five; upgrading lifts all four. Upgrade before the shoot.
- Alternatives: Telnyx SIP inbound local from $0.0032/min, toll-free $0.015/min, $12/month for the first ten channels; OVHcloud SIP trunk 4 € HT per channel per month, 19.99 € HT with unlimited calls, 9.99 € HT setup, French geographic numbers, per-number price not shown.
- Forwarding, France. Orange: Espace client "Vos services fixe > Renvoi d'appel" (espace-client.orange.fr/renvoi-appel), "si vous ne répondez pas ou si vous êtes déjà en ligne" plus a ring count, or "tous les appels, immédiatement, sans sonnerie"; handset `*21*<10 digits>#` / `#21#`, `*22*` / `#22#` voicemail on no answer, `*23*` / `#23#` voicemail when busy; also the Orange et moi app or the 3000; 0.111 € TTC per activation, deactivation free (unverified); forwarding to a mobile billed unless included; "Renvoi d'appels premium" forwards without ringing, by time slots, "en cascade", no price shown, incompatible with Stop Secret. Free: `*21*<numéro>#`, `#21#`, `*#21#`, 0.05 €/min on every forwarded minute to included destinations and 0.05 €/min plus the base rate elsewhere, callers hear a special tone; `*61*<numéro>#` with the delay set in the Espace client (5-20 s, and `*69*` for busy, unverified). Bouygues: `*21*`, `*61*`, `*69*`, four modes (all, no answer, busy, unavailable) in the app or web account, free to a landline, free to a mobile with unlimited calls, ring count up to 10 (unverified). SFR: same codes, `*21#` alone re-arms the last forward, the forwarded call is billed to the line that forwards (unverified).
- Forwarding, US. AT&T: `*72` / `*73` all calls, `*92 number #` / `*93#` no answer, `*90` / `*91` busy, `*372` / `*373` safe forwarding. Xfinity: connect.xfinity.com/voice/settings > Call Forwarding, voicemail after an adjustable ring count. Fios: My Verizon > Call Features, forwarding (all or selective) overrides voicemail on busy or no answer, out-of-network forwarded calls billed unless on the unlimited plan, forwarding cannot be on while Simultaneous Ring is on (unverified).
- Relative's steps: 1) read the operator from the box; 2) dial `*61*<Scam Shield number>#` (Orange: Espace client > Renvoi d'appel > "si vous ne répondez pas" plus ring count; AT&T `*92<number>#`; Xfinity "forward after N rings"; Fios Call Forwarding); 3) one test call. A US number can take a forwarded French call, but Free bills the international minute at base rate plus 0.05 €/min and Orange or Bouygues plans may not include US calls: use a French number or a mocked call for the French scene.
- Legal: only the caller is recorded, after the announcement, which makes it "au vu et au su" (226-1) and all-party-safe in the US states listed in feasibility #10; the senior is not on the call.
B. Voicemail-to-e-mail
- Free: Espace abonné (moncompte.free.fr) "Téléphonie > Messagerie vocale": alert e-mail, or alert with the sound file attached, up to four addresses; the file is WAV ADPCM A-law (CCITT) and not playable everywhere (ticket FS#18893, 2015, still open), so transcode before Muse Voice Transcribe; Freebox OS cannot fetch voicemail by API (FS#17112); sender undocumented; `#75#` off, `*75*5#` on, `**1` to listen.
- Orange: orange.fr "Vos services > Boîtes vocales > Mes paramètres > Notifications > Par e-mail"; only calls that leave a message; audio attachment and sender not stated.
- Bouygues (snippets and Bbox-Mag, unverified): the Bbox voicemail app closed 15 July 2026 (official snippet says 20 July); voicemail and forwarding preferences now in the Bouygues Telecom app or web account "Gérer ma ligne > Ma ligne fixe > Gérer et écouter ma messagerie vocale"; e-mail alerts under "Gérer ma ligne > Voir mon journal d'appels > Ajouter / Modifier mes e-mails" push the recording to the @bbox address or any registered address, format and sender not stated; listen on 661 or 09 81 66 16 61.
- SFR: no source for voicemail-to-e-mail; voicemail on 147, kept 15 days unheard, 48 h after listening, 7 days archived (unverified).
- US: Fios My Verizon > Voice Mail > Notification & Prompt Settings > "Attach Voice Mail in email alert", a .wav Verizon calls "not secure". Xfinity Voice Settings > Voicemail Notifications > "Enable voicemail transcription" plus an address: transcript in every e-mail, MP3 on request, transcripts capped at three minutes, delivered within a minute, sender not shown. AT&T Voicemail-to-Text (Unified Messaging account) forwards transcripts to e-mail, audio unspecified (portal: Voicemail Settings > Message settings > Message Transcription). Ooma Premier ($9.99/month or $119.99/year, Telo $79.99): e-mail or SMS with an optional audio file, my.ooma.com/preferences/voicemail, format unnamed (MP3, about 400 kB per minute, unverified). Vonage Voicemail Plus to e-mail, format unstated; magicJack subject tagged "Scam Likely" or "Verified" (unverified).
- Build: learn the recogniser (sender, subject) from one real sample per operator, detect the attachment, ffmpeg to a format Muse Voice Transcribe accepts, verdict card "Message left on your home phone"; latency is e-mail delivery, seconds to minutes. Fallback: a mocked e-mail in the demo inbox.
C. Operator checklist
- Orange Anti-spam appels fixe (Stop Pub renamed 10 Oct 2024): the caller hears "Merci de prononcer votre prénom suivi de votre nom", the subscriber hears "Vous avez un appel de [name]... Voulez-vous prendre cet appel" and answers "oui", "non" or "messagerie"; blocked and VIP lists in the Espace client "Internet et fixe > Votre fixe > Vos services fixe > Anti-spam appels fixe"; needs Orange Open or Livebox plus Orange Cybersecure (7 €/month at its June 2024 launch, unverified), or Stop Pub subscribed before 6 June 2024 (2 €/month, unverified), or inclusion in the plan; the phone must be plugged into the Livebox. Stop Secret: hidden-number callers identify themselves, identified calls show as 01 50 59 00 00, VIP numbers skip it, blocked numbers hear an unavailability message; free (unverified); Orange Open or Livebox only.
- Free: `*82#` / `#82#` / `*#82#` rejects anonymous calls (the caller hears a message), `*93#` rejects calls from forwarded lines, `*24#` plays an absence message; `*351#` and `*351*numéro#` blacklist (unverified); a robocall filter is an open request (FS#35300). SFR: `*82#` / `#82#`, box admin 192.168.1.1 "Téléphonie > Gérer vos options d'appels > Rejet des appels anonymes", blacklist via "Activation du filtrage" (unverified). Bouygues: anonymous blocking only in a 2021 forum answer (unverified).
- Xfinity Spam Blocker: free, on by default, cannot be turned off; High risk blocked without voicemail (portal log only), Medium to voicemail, Low gets an Audio CAPTCHA (the caller keys two digits, a pass rings through with a risk label); per-tier allow / filter / voicemail / block under Voice portal gear > Settings > Call Blocking. Spectrum Call Guard (snippets, unverified): free, on automatically, blocks known malicious numbers, labels "SPAM RISK", residential lines at the highest level, Allow List in Spectrum.net or the My Spectrum app, block list up to 30. AT&T Call Protect: Fraud Auto-block (busy signal) and Suspected Spam labels, on by default on AT&T Phone Advanced, opt-in on AT&T Phone via Features > Call filtering; Call Blocking `*61` per call (100 numbers, `*80` lifts it), Call Screening (only 20 chosen numbers ring, `*84#` off), Anonymous Call Blocking `*77#` / `*87#`, Do Not Disturb `*78#` / `*79#`. Fios Incoming Call Block: anonymous callers plus 10 numbers. Ooma Premier: suspected-spammer blocking from the Nomorobo database (800,000+ numbers), blocked callers hear a message, voicemail, a disconnected message or endless ringing, my.ooma.com/preferences/call_blocking; call screening plays the caller's recorded name and purpose. Vonage Call Filter (block, voicemail or allow SPAM-flagged calls) and Anonymous Call Block; magicJack block list of 99 numbers, blocked callers hear a busy signal (unverified).
- STIR/SHAKEN: mandatory on IP networks since 30 June 2021; an April 2025 NPRM targets the non-IP gap; FCC 26-17 notes number cycling still earns top attestation, so attestation is an input, never a verdict.
D. Box call-log feed
- Freebox OS API v4: `GET /api/v4/call/log/` returns `id`, `type` (missed, accepted, outgoing), `datetime`, `number`, `name`, `duration`, `new`, `contact_id`; `POST /api/v4/call/log/mark_all_as_read/`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/v4/call/log/{id}`; app token in `X-Fbx-App-Auth`, HTTPS mandatory, remote access through the box's `api_domain` and `https_port`; a WebSocket notification format exists with no event types listed; nothing on voicemail, forwarding or blocking.
- Livebox: no official API; the reverse-engineered sysbus interface (LiveboxMonitor v1.7, April 2026, Livebox 4 to 7, W7, S, admin password) lists received, missed and outgoing calls with time, number, contact and duration, no voicemail. Bbox: a 2017-2018 community client exposes `POST /login`, `GET /voip`, `GET /voip/fullcalllog/$line`, `PUT /voip/dial`; api.bbox.fr did not resolve.
E. Simultaneous-ring screener (US VoIP)
- Flow: the carrier rings the home phone and the Scam Shield number together; the webhook sees the caller number; a contact or allow-listed number gets `<Reject reason="busy">` as the first verb (the only way to leave a call unanswered and unbilled; `reason="rejected"` plays a not-in-service message), so the home phone keeps ringing; an unknown number is answered (the home phone rings once, as with Nomorobo), hears the greeting, `<Gather>` goes to Muse, then `<Dial callerId="<original caller>"><Number url="<whisper>">` rings the home line and plays `<Say>` of the verdict plus `<Gather>` "press 1 to take the call" before bridging (`<Say>`, `<Play>`, `<Gather>`, `<Hangup>` allowed in the whisper); `callerId` may be the inbound To or From, a Twilio number or a verified number; `timeout` 5-600 s (default 30 plus about 5 s); `action` receives `DialCallStatus` (completed, busy, no-answer, failed, canceled); `answerOnBridge` keeps the caller ringing; `machineDetection` available. A refused or unanswered whisper ends in a recorded message, a card and the relative's e-mail. The senior's phone rings 15-25 s after the first ring; on error connect the call.
- Simultaneous ring per provider: Xfinity Advanced Call Forwarding, up to five numbers total including the Xfinity number, connect.xfinity.com/voice/settings > Call Forwarding, secondary users need "Allow Access to Voice Features", simultaneous vs sequential not stated, 2024-2026 forum reports of a greyed-out feature (unverified); Fios Simultaneous Ring, three numbers, first answered wins, exclusive with Do Not Disturb, My Verizon > Manage your phone > Call Features; AT&T Locate Me, the line plus four numbers, att.com/support/home-phone/manage > Features > Feature Controls, `*313#` off; Vonage SimulRing, five additional phones; Ooma Multi-Ring on Premier; Spectrum Simultaneous Ring exists per a 2021 community thread and Call Guard can switch it off (unverified).
- Nomorobo: needs simultaneous ring; the free carrier-funded version ended 1 January 2026, now $39.99/year per line (mobile Basic $2.99/month or $19.99/year, Max $59.99/year, Family $79.99/year); copper lines lack simultaneous ring; Fios compatible but its one-click setup is gone; sign-up about five minutes; "139 supported VoIP carriers" (unverified); blocked calls ring once then go silent, check under a second (unverified); runs on Twilio. Verizon's Nomorobo page is a 404 and Xfinity's now serves the Spam Blocker page.
- Cost: $0.08-0.12 on Twilio for a 1-minute screen plus a 3-minute bridged call ($0.014-0.019/min on the bridged leg); ConversationRelay $0.07/min only for a multi-turn dialogue; Media Streams $0.0044/min; conference from $0.0018 per participant-minute; Conversation Intelligence $0.024 batch / $0.027 streaming per minute.
F. Unconditional forward
- `*21*<number>#` on the four French boxes (Orange "Renvoyer tous les appels, immédiatement, sans sonnerie" or the premium option), AT&T `*72`; Free bills 0.05 €/min on every call, Orange 0.111 € per activation (unverified), plus two Twilio legs; the Twilio fallback URL must `<Dial>` the home line when the server does not answer; caller-ID passthrough on French carriers unverified.

- France (Baromètre du numérique 2026, CREDOC, fieldwork June 2025, n = 4,145): 72% of households keep a fixed phone (89% in 2015), 80% of 60-69 and 85% of 70+, 47% never use one; 70+ are the least equipped with a smartphone (73%). Arcep Q2 2026: 35.7 M fixed subscriptions, 94% VoIP, RTC 2.248 M (-23.5% a year), 37 minutes a month per line (-7), 92% of fixed minutes on VoIP; RTC closure next wave 31 Jan 2027, finished end-2030 (unverified).

- trueCall (UK): Call Blocker £109.99 with 12 months of the control panel then £20/year, Secure £134.99 (older users, screens unknown callers), prices from cached shop pages; Carmarthenshire, Surrey and Buckinghamshire Trading Standards hand units out free.
- CPR Call Blocker V5000 $89.99, V100K $99.99 with 100,000 preloaded numbers (Walmart); Panasonic Call Block cordless KX-TG3812W $54.00, KX-TGD813B $87.98, KX-TG3824S $113.00 (Walmart).
- Amplicomms BigTel 1580 (big buttons, answering machine, 100-number blocker) £41.59; Doro Comfort 1015 about 37.88 € (stale listing), outgoing barring only, no inbound blacklist found.
- Lively Jitterbug Flip2 (US): mobile only, plans from $14.99/month, $35 activation, $79.99 device; owners cannot adjust call blocking.
