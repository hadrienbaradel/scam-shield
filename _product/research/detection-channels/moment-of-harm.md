# Moment of harm: call gate, link gate, native settings

What can step in when the senior dials back or taps a link, which native settings a relative can switch on, what each needs, how long it takes and whether it fits before Nov 4. Written 2026-10-28.

## Decision

Nov 4: build the Android link gate first (default-browser role, about 2 days), because one scene proves every channel at once (SMS, WhatsApp and email links all pass through it) and links are where the money goes; add the call gate (`CallRedirectionService`, 1.5-2 days) on Nov 1-2 only if the link gate is stable on the demo phone by Oct 31; write the "Protect this phone" checklist (0.5 day of content) in parallel and film one 10-second scene per item. After the event the same two gates are the product for real seniors (one system dialog each, no audio, no overlay, no AccessibilityService), with the install-first rule: the APK goes on before Advanced Protection or Auto Blocker, which block sideloaded installs and updates. iOS cannot intercept a dial or a tap (CallKit has no hook; the default-browser entitlement is managed and needs a native app), so iOS gets the checklist and encrypted DNS only. The payment moment stays content: the bank's Verification of Payee line in the French card and Play purchase approvals in the checklist.

## Build sheet

- Starting point: the companion declares `RECEIVE_SMS`, `INTERNET`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `SmsReceiver`, `MainActivity` and a non-exported `VerdictActivity`; no telecom service, no browser filter; minSdk 26, targetSdk 35, Compose, WorkManager. `CheckStore` seals 7 days of checks (sender, body, verdict) with a Keystore key; `VerdictActivity` has TTS EN/FR and the trusted-person loop; the server has `POST /api/check/text`, `/api/check/image` and `/api/devices/check`; `lib/detector.js` line 36 holds the US/FR phone regex.

**Call gate (Android 10+, API 29)**
- Manifest: `<service android:name=".call.CallGateService" android:exported="true" android:permission="android.permission.BIND_CALL_REDIRECTION_SERVICE">` with `<intent-filter><action android:name="android.telecom.CallRedirectionService"/></intent-filter>`; subclass `android.telecom.CallRedirectionService`. The deprecated `NEW_OUTGOING_CALL` broadcast would need the restricted `PROCESS_OUTGOING_CALLS` permission: not worth it.
- Role: `RoleManager.ROLE_CALL_REDIRECTION` ("android.app.role.CALL_REDIRECTION"), exclusive, the chooser offers "None", no extra permission; `isRoleAvailable`, then `isRoleHeld`, then `startActivityForResult(createRequestRoleIntent(...))`; without the role the service is never called. Settings path: Apps > Default apps > call redirection app (name varies by OEM; Samsung's chooser name unverified).
- Callback: `onPlaceCall(Uri handle, PhoneAccountHandle initialPhoneAccount, boolean allowInteractiveResponse)`, once per binding, never for emergency or gateway calls; reply only from it with `placeCallUnmodified()`, `cancelCall()`, `redirectCall(gatewayUri, targetPhoneAccount, confirmFirst)` or `placeCallToAlternateNumber(...)`; `onRedirectionTimeout()` fires when Telecom gives up.
- Timeout: Settings key `user_defined_call_redirection_timeout` = 5000 ms (carrier services also 5000 ms); no answer and Telecom cancels the call. Wrap the lookup in try/catch defaulting to `placeCallUnmodified()` and add a watchdog well under 5 s: a crash must never block the senior's calls.
- Screen: Telecom (the system service, not the dialer) binds with `BIND_AUTO_CREATE | BIND_FOREGROUND_SERVICE | BIND_ALLOW_BACKGROUND_ACTIVITY_STARTS`, so a plain `startActivity(CallWarningActivity)` is allowed from the bound service; start it before `cancelCall()`, since the privilege ends with the binding (the CVE-2023-40130 fix unbinds on `onBindingDied`). Fallbacks: a `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` overlay (user grant; the Play precedent uses it) or a full-screen intent notification (`USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` is limited to calling and alarm apps on Play for targetSdk 34+; sideloaded builds are not stated to lose it). Android 14/15 PendingIntent rules and Android 16 intent hardening do not touch a direct start; Android 11-16 document no API change.
- Logic: keep an index "number -> checkId, verdict, time" written when `CheckStore.save` runs, numbers taken with the detector's US/FR patterns (better: the server returns `phoneNumbers[]` in the verdict); normalise and look up locally (sub-millisecond, no network); unknown -> `placeCallUnmodified()` at once; `scam` or `be_careful` match -> `CallWarningActivity` (reuse `VerdictScreen`, TTS, "Don't call", "Call anyway", "Ask [trusted person]") then `cancelCall()`; "Call anyway" puts the number in a 60-second approved set and fires `ACTION_DIAL` (no permission, the senior presses the green button) or `ACTION_CALL` (`CALL_PHONE`); `allowInteractiveResponse == false` (car mode): let the call through and post a notification. False positives are bounded: only numbers from a message Scam Shield already judged.
- Quirks and precedents: Galaxy S22 and S10 (Android 12) never pass numbers starting with "08" to the service (treated as potential emergency numbers) and Samsung's confirmation dialog may omit the app name (both forum, unverified); emulator support unverified, plan a physical phone. Play precedent "Call Confirmation" (`ch.viaccoz.callconfirmation`, 4.0 stars) asks for the role plus Display over other apps and warns of the 5-second timeout; reviews report silent failures and removed call-confirm apps (anecdotal). Play policy names neither the service nor the role; the closest exception is "Caller ID, spam detection, and/or spam blocking". Sideloaded: moot.
- Relative's steps: 1) Scam Shield > Protect this phone > "Warn me before I call a number from a scam text"; 2) system dialog "Set Scam Shield as your default call redirection app?" > Allow. Fallback: the verdict card names the number not to call.
- Effort 1.5-2 days: 0.5 service, role and index (JVM-testable like `RegexPreScreenerTest`); 0.5 warning screen; 0.5-1 on a phone (cancel and re-dial, Samsung, FR number formats).
- iOS: CallKit covers the app's own VoIP calls, Call Directory caller ID and blocking of incoming numbers, `CXCallObserver`, and the default calling app since iOS 18.2; nothing intercepts or rewrites a dialed number, and recently dialed numbers are exempt from Call Directory blocking (Apple DTS, unverified). Screen Time Communication Limits is the only system feature that restricts outgoing calls.

**Link gate (Android, `ROLE_BROWSER`)**
- Manifest: an exported activity with `<intent-filter><action android:name="android.intent.action.VIEW"/><category android:name="android.intent.category.DEFAULT"/><category android:name="android.intent.category.BROWSABLE"/><data android:scheme="http"/><data android:scheme="https"/></intent-filter>` and no host, so `handleAllWebDataURI` is true; `BrowserRoleBehavior` rejects non-exported or host-qualified candidates.
- Role: `RoleManager.ROLE_BROWSER` ("android.app.role.BROWSER", API 29), exclusive; `createRequestRoleIntent(ROLE_BROWSER)` shows "Set Scam Shield as your default browser app?", or Settings > Apps > Default apps > Browser app; grants `PROVIDE_OWN_AUTOFILL_SUGGESTIONS` on API 34+. The user or "reset app preferences" can take it back silently: show the state on the Protect page.
- Routing (Android 12+): a generic web intent reaches an app only when it is approved for that domain (App Links verified through `https://host/.well-known/assetlinks.json` with `android:autoVerify="true"`, or user association under Open by default; one app per domain), otherwise it goes to the default browser. Links to verified apps' domains (bank, Amazon, PayPal, YouTube) bypass the gate: a feature. Blind spots: in-app browsers (Messenger, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) and WebViews; WhatsApp and Messenger defaults unverified.
- Custom Tabs: a Custom Tab is `ACTION_VIEW` with extras; `CustomTabsClient.getPackageName` prefers the default VIEW handler "as long as it supports Custom Tabs", so without a `CustomTabsService` those apps skip Scam Shield and open Chrome. Stub: an `androidx.browser` `CustomTabsService` whose `warmup` and `newSession` return true (+0.5 day; test with Gmail, whose use of Custom Tabs is unverified). Android 11+ callers need `<queries>` entries.
- Hand-off: `Intent(ACTION_VIEW, url).setPackage("com.android.chrome")`, fallback any browser package found through `<queries>`; never a bare `ACTION_VIEW` (it loops back). Never render the page (Play abuse policy example 10 targets WebViews loading untrusted URLs with a JS interface); Chrome Safe Browsing still runs after the hand-off.
- Check: `POST /api/check/text` with the URL as text (or a new `/api/devices/check-link`) runs the link rules (RDAP age, address signals, redirects) and Muse; instant pass-through for the senior's own brands and a top-sites allowlist (no network); 3-second cap, then "Still checking, open anyway?"; "Open anyway" always visible.
- Precedents: LinkSheet (GitHub nightlies, "reimplements the pre-Android 12 link handling", lists verified-link apps to disable), Better Open With (on Play per press, unverified), Open Link With (Play listing 404 on 2026-10-28, reason unknown). Play policy has no browser-specific rule.
- Relative's steps: 1) Scam Shield > Protect this phone > "Check links before they open"; 2) dialog "Set Scam Shield as your default browser app?" > Allow. Fallback: the share sheet, or pasting the link into Scam Shield.
- Effort about 2 days: 0.5 filters, role and hand-off with `<queries>`; 0.5 server call and verdict mapping (reuse `StoredCheck` and `Verdict`); 0.5 card EN/FR with TTS; 0.5 testing from Google Messages, WhatsApp and Gmail on the demo phone; +0.5 Custom Tabs stub.
- iOS: default browser since iOS 14 behind the managed entitlement `com.apple.developer.web-browser` ("Default browser entitlement request form"; one refusal reported, unverified). The app must declare HTTP and HTTPS in Info.plist, must not use UIWebView, must show on launch a URL field, search tools or bookmarks, must "navigate directly to the specified destination", may present a "Safe Browsing or other warning" for suspected phishing, may not claim Universal Links for specific domains, and is rejected if it declares photo-library, always-on location, HomeKit, background Bluetooth or Health usage keys; a non-WebKit engine needs BrowserEngineKit (EU only, iOS 17.4+, 90% of Web Platform Tests, 80% of Test262). The only system-wide filter is encrypted DNS: `NEDNSSettingsManager` (iOS 14+, the user enables it in Settings) or a configuration profile pointing at a filtering resolver; no card.

**Protect this phone checklist**
- A page in the companion and the web app (EN/FR), one screenshot per step, done by the relative in person or over FaceTime: remote management exists only for Child Accounts under Family Sharing on iOS and not at all on Android (Advanced Protection and Auto Blocker are device-local).
- Order rule: install the APK and plan its updates before Advanced Protection or Auto Blocker; a first sideload is also blocked during a call with a non-contact on Android 16. Developer verification does not bite in the US or France before 2027 (Sept 30, 2026 rollout: Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand; limited distribution accounts cover 20 devices).
- Automatic, nothing to switch: Android 16 blocks a first sideload, accessibility grants and disabling Play Protect during calls with non-contacts (Phone by Google dependency unverified), warns with a 30-second pause when a bank app opens during screen sharing (UK pilot), and 2026 adds "Verified financial calls" (Android 11+, Revolut, Itaú, Nubank first); iOS 26 Call Screening turns off for 24 h after an emergency call and while roaming. Skip Lockdown Mode (blocks links in Messages and most attachments; Apple calls it extreme).
- Present Google's, Samsung's and Apple's features as allies; Scam Shield keeps the senior's own life as context, one plain verdict in EN/FR, the trusted person, Gmail and the two gates.

**Payment moment (content only; nothing buildable by Nov 4)**
- EU: since 9 October 2025 every SEPA transfer, classic or instant, carries Verification of Payee (regulation 2024/886): "correspondance exacte", "partielle", "aucune correspondance", "vérification impossible"; the customer may still pay, and the bank is then no longer liable; non-euro SEPA countries by 9 July 2027. French card line: "If your bank shows 'aucune correspondance', stop."
- US: FINRA Rule 4512(a)(1)(F) makes brokerages ask for a trusted contact (18+, contacted about possible financial exploitation, cannot transact or see balances); no US confirmation-of-payee mandate found; deposit accounts unverified.
- Open banking sees a new payee hours later: Plaid checks 1-4 times a day per institution (`SYNC_UPDATES_AVAILABLE` webhook; `/transactions/refresh` is billed with no freshness promise); Powens wants webhooks first and allows a forced sync when the user opens the app, no interval published; Bridge fires `item.refreshed` per refresh (unverified).
- Play purchase approvals: the family manager picks "All content", "All purchases that use the family payment method", "Only in-app purchases" or "No approval required"; Play billing only. Apple's Ask to Buy is child-account only; no adult option.

## Corrections to feasibility.md

- #6: Sideloading has new blockers: Android 16 Advanced Protection and Samsung Auto Blocker block installs and updates of sideloaded apps once on; Android 16 blocks a first sideload during a call with a non-contact. Install the APK first.
- #7: iOS 26 Screen Unknown Senders already hides unknown texts and blocks their links until "Mark as Known", natively; the third-party filter extension never applies to senders the senior replied to three or more times.
- #8: Outgoing calls are interceptable on Android 10+: `CallRedirectionService` with `ROLE_CALL_REDIRECTION` sees every non-emergency dialed number, has 5 s to cancel, redirect or pass it, may start a screen, no audio; iOS has no outgoing hook.
- #13: Two interrupt paths need no overlay or AccessibilityService: a Telecom-bound `CallRedirectionService` may start an activity from the background, and the browser role catches every tapped link; Play limits `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` to calling and alarm apps (targetSdk 34+).
