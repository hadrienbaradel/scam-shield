# Voicemail routes into Scam Shield
How a senior's voicemails can reach Scam Shield per carrier and phone (France and US), what each route needs, how long it takes and whether it fits before Nov 4; written 2026-10-28, all sources accessed that day.

## Decision
For Nov 4 build route A (the senior shares the voicemail audio; the server transcribes with Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Spark judges): one day, any phone and carrier, two Meta models on screen. Add route B (carrier voicemail-to-email read by the Gmail sync) only if a Free or Orange line or a sample email is in hand, since it is the only zero-tap story. Route C (forwarding to a Twilio number) only with an upgraded account and a US number; route D (answering agent) only if A and B are done by Nov 1. After the hackathon: France runs on B (Free, Orange and SFR email the audio for 0-2 €/month; Bouygues cannot) with A as the universal fallback and C/D held until a French number and a legal review exist; the US runs on C (any carrier, one-time codes, plan minutes) with D as the premium tier and E (Google Voice) for Google households.

## Build sheet

### A. Share or upload the audio (1 day server, +0.5 Android, +0.5 iOS)
- Server: `POST /api/check/audio` (multipart, any ffmpeg input: .m4a from iOS, .wav from Free, MP3/WAV from Twilio or Orange) → `ffmpeg -i in -ac 1 -ar 16000 -c:a pcm_s16le -map_metadata -1 out.wav` → `/v1/asr/transcribe` → the existing verdict pipeline with a `voicemail` message type and caller metadata; `/api/check/image` is the model (never stored).
- Android: companion `ACTION_SEND` target for `audio/*` posting to `/api/devices/check`. The Google Phone app shares the audio with "Send to" and the transcript with "Share" (transcripts English and Spanish only, Android 8.0+, listed US carriers; a carrier VVM app turns the Phone app's voicemail off). Orange, Bouygues and Free visual voicemail apps share audio; Orange on iPhone shares "par SMS, email ou Fichiers".
- iOS: no API. Phone > Voicemail > message > Share gives an `.m4a` (third-party guides (unverified); size cap and codec unknown); a share-sheet Shortcut (input type Media; "Get Contents of URL", POST, body File) uploads it. Transcript text: long-press, extend the selection, Share (unverified).
- Senior: 1) open the voicemail, 2) tap Share or Send to, 3) pick Scam Shield; the card shows the transcript, the ask, "the callback number 09 70... is not Crédit Agricole's" (`lib/context.js`), one action, "Ask Hadrien".
- Cost per 1-minute voicemail: $0.003 transcription plus about $0.01 verdict. Fallback: paste the transcript into "Check something else".

### B. Carrier voicemail-to-email read by the Gmail sync (1-2 days)
- Free Mobile, all plans: Espace Abonné Mobile > Mon forfait mobile > Messagerie vocale > Notification; mode "simple" (caller number, date, duration) or "avec fichier audio" (`.wav` attached); up to 10 addresses; sender address not published; option documented since at least Jan 2023.
- Orange 888: from orange.fr only: Vos services > Boîtes vocales > Paramétrer la notification par E-mail > mes préférences > notification > activer > address > valider; the email carries the caller number and the sound file (page updated 14/06/2024); an Orange forum says MP3 to the Orange mailbox and one user could not forward it to Gmail (unverified). SMS alerts are on by default unless visual voicemail is active.
- SFR Répondeur+: 2 €/month on RED, included in SFR plans; Android 7.0+ app required, iOS gets visual voicemail automatically; activation about 1 h; "recevoir également vers l'email de votre choix"; format and iOS email setting unstated (unverified: sfr.fr blocks crawlers).
- Bouygues: email alerts exist only for the Bbox fixed line; mobile voicemail 660 has none (unverified, search snippets); visual voicemail on by default; `#002#` clears every forwarding; iOS 18+ runs Apple voicemail alongside 660.
- US: none of the big three emails the audio. Verizon: voicemail and visual voicemail included; Premium Visual Voicemail (Android) and Voicemail to Text for iPhone (SMS, off when Live Voicemail is on) $2.99/line/month, not prepaid (unverified), first 45 s transcribed, forwarding by text or email manual and Premium-only. AT&T wireless: iPhone transcripts (iOS 10+), Pixel transcription switch (Google's), email forwarding only on U-verse Voice landlines. T-Mobile: Voicemail to Text inside Scam Shield Premium (price unverified), to the VVM app or SMS, primary account holder enrolls, prepaid excluded, "Send as SMS" switch (snippets, unverified). Google Fi: "Voicemails by text", English only, MP3s via Takeout, no email.
- Server: match sender plus `audio/*` attachment (`attachmentsOf` in `src/google/gmail.js` keeps name, type and size only; `lib/muse.js` passes names and types), fetch with `messages.attachments.get`, ffmpeg, ASR, verdict with the caller number parsed from the body: "received 2 minutes ago, judged before you listened".
- Relative: 1) log in to the senior's Free or Orange account, 2) open the voicemail notification setting, 3) tick the audio attachment, 4) enter the senior's Gmail address. Fallback: replay a sample Free email through the mock inbox.

### C. Conditional forwarding to a Twilio number with `<Record>` (2-3 days US; FR adds the bundle)
- GSM codes: `*61*<num>#` no answer (`*61*<num>**<sec>#` or `*61*<num>*11*<sec>#` sets the delay, 05-30 s on some networks), `*62*` unreachable, `*67*` busy, `*004*` all conditional, `*21*` all calls; `**` registers, `#` cancels, `##` deregisters, `*#` queries. Resellers may not support conditional forwarding.
- US: AT&T `*61*`/`*62*`/`*67*<num>#`, off `##61#`/`##62#`/`##67#`, forwarded calls billed for their length (some plans exclude them from plan minutes), no international, set from the phone only. Verizon `*71<num>` (busy or unanswered after 3-6 rings), `*72` all, `*73` off, no international, charges unstated; forwarding can stop voicemail. T-Mobile `**61*1<num>#` or `**61*<num>**<sec>#` (5-second steps up to 30 s), off `##61#`, `##004#` clears all, no international (snippets, unverified).
- France: no carrier forwards abroad, so a French number is mandatory. Free: four types, metropolitan 01-05/09 and 06/07 only, "Option Renvoi d'appels métropolitains" 0.05 €/min (tariffs from 21 July 2026), own voicemail free, codes not on the page. Bouygues: `*61*`/`*62*`/`*67*<num>#`, off `#61#`/`#62#`/`#67#`, `#002#` clears all, free option, Bouygues or French fixed destinations from the plan, other French numbers out-of-plan (one 2025 article: 0.50 €/min), billed to whoever set it (third-party guide, 28 Apr 2026, unverified). Orange: only `**62*<num>*11#` documented (status `*#62#`, `##002#` clears); Pro bundles absorb forwarded traffic; consumer no-answer code and billing unverified. SFR: `*21*`/`*69*`/`*61*<num>#`, cancel `#21#`/`#69#`/`#61#`, billing unstated (third-party, unverified).
- Twilio: the call webhook gives `From` (E.164, or anonymous/unknown), `ForwardedFrom` (only when the carrier passes it: test per carrier), `CallerName` with `VoiceCallerIdLookup` (Lookup pricing). TwiML: `<Say>` greeting, then `<Record maxLength="120" playBeep="true" finishOnKey="#" recordingStatusCallback="...">` (defaults 3600 s, any key, beep on); the callback posts `RecordingUrl`, `RecordingDuration`, `RecordingStatus` (`completed` or `failed`); WAV by default (8 kHz mono from μ-law, 128 kbps), `.mp3` on request. Twilio's own transcription is American English only, 2-120 s, $0.05/min: skip it; resample with ffmpeg as in A.
- Prices (Aug 2026): US local number $1.15/month (toll-free $2.15), inbound $0.0085/min (toll-free $0.022), outbound $0.014, recording $0.0025/min, storage $0.0005/min/month, Conversation Intelligence $0.024/min batch, $0.027 streaming; France local number $1.35/month, inbound $0.01/min, outbound to French mobiles $0.0404-0.1603/min (national and mobile number prices not shown). About $0.025 per 1-minute voicemail plus the carrier leg (US plan minutes; Free 0.05 €; Bouygues out-of-plan).
- French number: regulatory bundle; individual: ID, passport or residence permit plus an address proof in France (ID with local address, utility bill, tax notice, rent receipt, title deed; no PO Box); business: K-bis plus RCS/SIREN; toll-free business-only with an EEA address; review time unpublished, French mobile numbers about 1-2 weeks (unverified). Telnyx: same plus proof dated within 3 months and physical presence in France at purchase. Vonage: 06/07 caller ID person-to-person only. OVH: carrier trunk for ARCEP-registered operators only.
- Trial: 75 voice minutes, lapses after 30 days, calls only to or from up to 5 verified numbers in the sign-up country, every call plays "You have a trial account" and waits for a keypress, 10-minute cap (unverified); `<Record>` on trial unverified. Upgrade before recording.
- One number serves many seniors only if `ForwardedFrom` arrives; otherwise one number per senior ($1.15-1.35/month) and the mapping comes from `To`. All three forwards replace the carrier voicemail; forwarding no-answer only splits messages across two mailboxes. iOS Live Voicemail must be off: the phone answers first and the network never sees "no answer" (third-party, unverified).
- Relative: 1) upgrade Twilio, buy a number, point its voice webhook at the tunnel; 2) on the senior's phone dial `*61*<num>#`, `*62*<num>#`, `*67*<num>#` (or `**004*<num>#`); 3) turn Live Voicemail off; 4) call the senior to test. Fallback: route A.

### D. Answering agent (ConversationRelay, 3-5 days)
- TwiML `<Connect><ConversationRelay url="wss://..." language="fr-FR" welcomeGreeting="...">`: the WebSocket delivers transcribed "prompt" messages, the server answers with text tokens that are spoken. STT Google or Deepgram (Deepgram default for accounts created after 12 Sep 2025), TTS ElevenLabs by default (also Google, Amazon); defaults en-US ElevenLabs `UgBBYS2sOqTuMpoF3BR0`, fr-FR ElevenLabs `a5n9pJUnAhX4fn7lx3uo`, speech model `telephony`, Google STT; 36 default locales incl. fr-CA; `multi` auto-detects the language (Deepgram plus ElevenLabs only); barge-in on by default; `speechTimeout` 600-5000 ms; DTMF events optional; the AI addendum must be accepted. Deepgram fr-FR on nova-3 unstated.
- Price $0.07/min plus voice minutes: about $0.08-0.12/min all-in, a 45-second screen under $0.10 plus the carrier leg. Twilio publishes no latency; per turn STT endpointing 0.5-1 s + Muse Spark at minimal/low 3-5 s + TTS start 0.3 s, about 4-6 s of silence: fine for a scripted two-question screen ("Who is calling? What is it about?"), not free conversation; put the first question in `welcomeGreeting` so it costs no model call.
- Disclosure: EU AI Act art. 50(1), in force 2 Aug 2026 (callers must be told they talk to an AI); French 226-1 (open recording); FCC 24-17 of 2 Feb 2024 treats AI voices as artificial under the TCPA (outbound; FCC 24-84 of July 2024 proposed an AI announcement, status unchecked); California SB 1001 (bots influencing a purchase or vote, since 1 July 2019). Greeting: "You have reached Marie's assistant. This call is recorded and handled by an AI assistant that will take your message." Scammers who hang up are a feature.
- Benchmark: iOS 26 Call Screening (name and reason before the phone rings; Never / Ask Reason for Calling / Silence; skipped while roaming and 24 h after an emergency call) and Pixel 10 "Take a Message" (Oct 2025, on-device, lives in the call log, German beta in 2026, no API) do this without the senior's context or the trusted-person loop. Fallback: route C.

### E. Google Voice as the voicemail (0.5-1 day on top of B)
- voice.google.com > Settings > Voicemail > "Get voicemail via email"; the email comes from `voice-noreply@google.com` with "Voicemail from: <name> <number> at <time>", the transcript and a link to the MP3 (sign-in required, no attachment) (secondary source, unverified). Personal tier US-only; the 14-country Workspace footprint incl. France needs a paid Voice add-on; transcription languages documented for Workspace only (English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Portuguese, Spanish). Carrier codes per Google's page (AT&T `*61*`, Verizon `*71`). Fallback: G.

### F. Android notification listener (1-2 days)
- `NotificationListenerService` bound with `BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`; the senior enables Notification access (Android 13+ sideload: "Allow restricted settings" first (unverified)). Caller info sits in the notification text and the transcript in the big-text section (AOSP fork commit, unverified). Transcripts arrive only when the carrier's OMTP server sends them (OMTP 1.3 plain-text attachments): Charter-Verizon, Comcast-Xfinity, Freedom, O2, T-Mobile Pixel, Tracfone, Visible; English and Spanish.

### G. Carrier voicemail-to-text (0 days)
- Verizon Voicemail to Text SMS (first 45 s) lands in the Android companion's SMS path and is judged as a text; T-Mobile Scam Shield Premium (price unverified); AT&T iPhone transcripts stay in the Phone app.

### H and I. On-phone access
- Android: `READ_VOICEMAIL` is `signature|privileged|role` (default dialer only), `ADD_VOICEMAIL` is dangerous and shows only the app's own entries (official reference via snippets); the OMTP client (Android 6.0+) lives in `packages/services/Telephony` and fills the provider for any Dialer; a carrier VVM app can disable it; the default dialer gets the voicemail permission set (AICP Dialer source, secondary). A full dialer: 2-4 weeks (unverified).
- iOS: no voicemail framework; Live Voicemail (Settings > Apps > Phone) and Call Screening are listed for 26 locales incl. English (United States) and French (France), Voicemail Summaries for 19; Live Voicemail transcribes on the Neural Engine and falls back to the carrier mailbox when the phone is off; Verizon stops SMS transcripts while it is on; one MVNO advises turning it off when forwarding misbehaves (unverified).

### Transcription (all routes)
- `muse-voice-transcribe-1.0`, `POST /v1/asr/transcribe`, multipart: part `request` (JSON: `mode` PUSH_TO_TALK, ENDPOINTING or DIARIZATION; `model`; `audioEncoding: "WAV"`; `languageBias`; `keywords`) and part `audio`: RIFF/WAVE, mono, 16-bit PCM, 16 or 24 kHz only; over 32 MB → 413, over 10 minutes → 400; response `transcript`, `audioDurationMs`, `turns[{turnId, startMs, endMs, transcript, speaker}]`; `Accept: text/event-stream` streams partials. Realtime `wss://api.meta.ai/v1/asr/realtime`: raw 16-bit mono PCM (`PCM_24KHZ` preferred, `PCM_16KHZ` resampled), handshake within 10 s, sessions up to 60 minutes, paced near real time (route D only). Meta's conversion: `ffmpeg -i input.m4a -ac 1 -ar 24000 -c:a pcm_s16le -map_metadata -1 output.wav`.
- 25 languages with code-switching (Arabic, Bengali, Dutch, English, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Kannada, Korean, Malay, Mandarin, Marathi, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish, Tagalog, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Turkish, Vietnamese); diarization, endpointing, keyword biasing, turn-level timestamps; no word timestamps, no TTS on the API. $0.18/h ($0.003/min) for file and realtime, billed in whole seconds, zero-data-retention at the same price; no RPM/TPM caps, 256 concurrent streams, 16,000 streams/hour shared. No published per-language accuracy.
- Seed `languageBias: ["French","English"]` and `keywords` from the senior's context (bank, carrier, grandchildren's first names): a concrete Meta-tooling talking point. Muse Spark 1.3 Standard: $1.25 in / $0.15 cached / $4.25 out per 1M tokens, about $0.01 per verdict; 1,000 seniors x 2 voicemails/day ≈ $26/day.
- Muse Spark audio input: MP3 or WAV as an `input_audio` part (base64 `data` plus `format`; the Responses API also takes an `audio_url` data URI or a `file_id`), examples on `muse-spark-1.2`, "not fully supported" on 1.3, `max_tokens` at least 4000 or the transcript returns empty with `finish_reason: length`; no size or duration limit published. Keep Voice Transcribe → 1.3 text verdict so the demo line stays "Muse Spark 1.3".
- Fallbacks: OpenAI `gpt-transcribe` $0.0045/min (`gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` $0.003, `gpt-4o-transcribe` and Whisper $0.006, live `gpt-live-transcribe` $0.017/min; `whisper-1`, `gpt-4o-transcribe`, `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` and `gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize` shut down 26 Feb 2027; mp3/mp4/mpeg/mpga/m4a/wav/webm up to 25 MB; French). Deepgram Nova-3 $0.0043/min mono, $0.0052 multilingual (streaming $0.0048/$0.0058 promo, list $0.0077/$0.0092), $200 free credit, 45+ languages. Twilio Conversation Intelligence $0.024/min batch, $0.027 streaming (languages unchecked).
