# Android hooks beyond RECEIVE_SMS

Which Android system integrations the companion can add, what each sees, what the senior enables, policy and version limits, effort, and what fits before Nov 4. Written 2026-10-28; day figures are the researcher's estimates for this team on the existing Kotlin app (minSdk 26, targetSdk 35, no `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`, no `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`).

## Decision

For Nov 4: add the full-screen intent to `VerdictActivity` (0.5 day), a notification listener for Messages RCS, WhatsApp, Messenger and Telegram (1.5-2 days), and the share target plus PROCESS_TEXT (under 1 day); all run on the sideloaded APK with at most one Settings toggle each and no review. The default digital assistant (2-3 days) is the stretch scene once the listener lands: one long-press hands Muse Spark a screenshot and the text of any app. Call screening (1 day) only if a day is left. After the demo, a file-installed APK on Android 15/16 needs one "Allow restricted settings" tap from a relative, and a Play build must drop `RECEIVE_SMS` and rely on the listener, the share target and optionally the assistant role (a permitted SMS use on Play); never the default SMS role, which would remove Google Messages and its on-device scam detection.

## Build sheet

Restricted settings (sideloaded APK, Android 13-16)
- Android 15 and 16 CDD [9.8/H-0-1]: for an app installed from a local file (`PACKAGE_SOURCE_LOCAL_FILE`) or a non-store download (`PACKAGE_SOURCE_DOWNLOADED_FILE`) the device MUST block, until confirmed from the app's App info page (`EnhancedConfirmationManager`, [H-0-4]): accessibility, notification listener, device admin, display over other apps, usage access, the Dialer and SMS roles, and the SMS runtime permission group; on by default [H-0-2]. Android 13 and 14 blocked only accessibility and notification listener. Full-screen notifications, "turn screen on", install unknown apps and alarms are only STRONGLY RECOMMENDED [H-0-5]; AOSP's `PROTECTED_SETTINGS` set (SEND/RECEIVE/READ_SMS, RECEIVE_MMS, RECEIVE_WAP_PUSH, READ_CELL_BROADCASTS, the SMS group, device admin, accessibility, notifications, SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, usage stats, ROLE_DIALER, ROLE_SMS, flagged REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES) excludes the full-screen-intent op; Samsung may differ (unverified).
- `adb install` tags the package `PACKAGE_SOURCE_OTHER` and allowlists all restricted permissions (`INSTALL_ALL_WHITELIST_RESTRICTED_PERMISSIONS`), so the emulator flow never shows the prompt and `pm grant` works; pre-installed or allow-listed installers are exempt, and whether a shipping Pixel or Galaxy treats a null installer as trusted depends on its `enhanced-confirmation.xml` (no source). RECEIVE_SMS and READ_SMS are hard-restricted: held only once the installer on record allowlists them (the package installer does for file installs).
- Relative's steps, once per app: 1) Settings > Apps > See all apps > Scam Shield, 2) three-dot menu > Allow restricted settings, 3) confirm, then grant the permission. Before that the toggle is greyed with "for your security, this setting is currently unavailable" (unverified wording). Debug unlock: `adb shell cmd appops set com.scamshield.companion ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS allow`. Truecaller documents the same step for APK installs. Put the step in the pairing page and the README.

Notification listener
- Service: subclass `NotificationListenerService`, `android:permission="android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE"` (signature level, only the system binds), action `android.service.notification.NotificationListenerService`; optional meta-data `android.service.notification.default_filter_types` (`conversations|alerting`, API 31) and `disabled_filter_types` (`ongoing|silent`). Work only after `onListenerConnected()`; `requestRebind` fails until granted; never bound on low-RAM devices on Android 10 and below; work-profile listeners are ignored; `getActiveNotifications()` lists what is visible to the current user.
- Data: `EXTRA_TITLE`, `EXTRA_TEXT`, `EXTRA_BIG_TEXT`, `EXTRA_TEXT_LINES`; MessagingStyle apps (Google Messages SMS/MMS/RCS, WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram) carry `EXTRA_MESSAGES` and `EXTRA_HISTORIC_MESSAGES`, parsed with `Notification.MessagingStyle.Message.getMessagesFromBundleArray` (API 30+) into `getText()` and `getSenderPerson()`; skip `FLAG_GROUP_SUMMARY` and own notifications, dedupe by notification key. WhatsApp stacked summaries show the app name as title and a count line (unverified); Gmail shows sender as title, account as subText, subject plus snippet as text (unverified); RCS text does arrive through Google Messages notifications (Tasker forum, unverified); Phone app gives "Missed call" with number or name, and Pixel 6+ Take a Message shows the caller name plus the opening words of the transcript in supported regions (live transcription: US, UK, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Ireland, Canada, Australia, India, Japan). Media never arrives, only "Photo" or "Voice message" placeholders; muted chats and blocked channels are never posted (unverified); nothing is seen before the listener binds.
- Android 15+ OTP redaction, all apps regardless of targetSdk: a listener without `RECEIVE_SENSITIVE_NOTIFICATIONS` (signature|preinstalled|knownSigner|role; held by the HOME, COMPANION_DEVICE_WATCH/COMPUTER/GLASSES, SYSTEM_UI and notification-intelligence roles, not by ASSISTANT, SMS or CALL_SCREENING) receives "sensitive notification content hidden" in place of any notification in which an OTP is detected, so "share code 123456 with our agent" texts are invisible to it. Android 16 adds lock-screen OTP masking off Wi-Fi with no recent unlock (unverified); detection patterns are undocumented.
- Senior setup: store install 2 taps (Settings > Notifications > Device & app notifications or Notification access > Scam Shield > Allow); sideloaded 13+ adds "Allow restricted settings" first. Deep link `Settings.ACTION_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SETTINGS`; no programmatic grant.
- Play: the sensitive-permissions policy has no notification-access section and no declaration form; prominent-disclosure guidance names Accessibility, background location and package visibility, not listeners; treat it like Accessibility (in-app disclosure, affirmative consent). Play Protect "enhanced fraud protection" blocks sideloaded installs that request RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS, notification access or Accessibility in Singapore, Thailand, Brazil, India, then Vietnam, South Africa, Philippines, Nigeria, Kenya, Hong Kong (unverified); not the US or France in any source.
- Precedents: Bitdefender Scam Protection (Accessibility, notification access and SMS; warns before a link is tapped, cannot delete texts, paid), Truecaller Caller ID for WhatsApp (notification access, says it does not read content), Google's Pixel "Scam Detection in notifications" (Sep 15, 2026, Pixel 6+, US, Australia, Canada, France, Germany, India, Japan, Mexico, Singapore, UK; English, Arabic, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish) (unverified). Non-listener rivals: Norton Safe Social (WhatsApp linked device, US, English), Malwarebytes Text Protection (overlay plus scan texts), McAfee Text Scam Detector.
- Build, 1.5-2 days: `ScamNotificationListener` with a package allowlist, run `PreScreener`, enqueue `CheckSmsWorker` with a `source` field; `source` on `/api/devices/check` and in the verdict copy ("a WhatsApp message from +33 6..."); one onboarding card with both deep links. Emulator: `adb shell cmd notification post -S bigtext -t "Mom" tag "text"` synthesizes a notification (unverified on this image); one real-phone WhatsApp take. Fallback: the share target.

Text selection (ACTION_PROCESS_TEXT) and share target (ACTION_SEND)
- One exported `CheckTextActivity`: filter action `android.intent.action.PROCESS_TEXT`, category DEFAULT, mimeType `text/plain`; read `EXTRA_PROCESS_TEXT` with `Bundle.getCharSequence()` (may be styled), `EXTRA_PROCESS_TEXT_READONLY` (API 23); no permission or setting. Toolbar since Android 6.0; Compose text fields expose it since Foundation 1.9.0 (`ProcessTextKey`).
- Gmail and Keep hide it (Google issue 340098899); workaround: a second filter on the same activity with action VIEW, category BROWSABLE, data scheme https. Chrome works; WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram and Outlook unverified; apps that block selection give nothing. AOSP main carries the flag `ignore_process_text` (namespace `permissions`, bug 325356776), "an experiment about deprecating PROCESS_TEXT" that empties the resolver list; no evidence it is on in any shipping build.
- Share: the same activity with `ACTION_SEND` for `text/plain` (`EXTRA_TEXT`) and `image/*` (`EXTRA_STREAM`), multipart upload to `/api/check/image` through the device token. Effort: PROCESS_TEXT 2-4 hours, text share 1 hour, image share 0.5-1 day. Senior: long-press, select, "..." overflow is a fine-motor task; "Share > Scam Shield" is easier and is the fallback for every other hook.

Default digital assistant (ROLE_ASSISTANT)
- Components: `VoiceInteractionService` (permission `BIND_VOICE_INTERACTION`, signature) with meta-data `android.voice_interaction` XML naming `sessionService`, a stub `recognitionService` and `supportsAssist=true`, plus `VoiceInteractionSessionService` and `VoiceInteractionSession`; or an activity for `android.intent.action.ASSIST`. Role `android.app.role.ASSISTANT` (API 29) is exclusive, showNone and `requestable="false"`: no in-app dialog, Settings only; it grants the `sms` permission set, READ_CALL_LOG, EXECUTE_APP_ACTION (API 34), EMBED_ANY_APP_IN_UNTRUSTED_MODE (API 35) and the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW app-op.
- Session: `onShow(args, flags)` requesting `SHOW_WITH_ASSIST` (1) and `SHOW_WITH_SCREENSHOT` (2); sources `SHOW_SOURCE_ASSIST_GESTURE` (4), `SHOW_SOURCE_PUSH_TO_TALK` (32); `onHandleScreenshot(Bitmap)` is null when the user, a policy or FLAG_SECURE disables it; `onHandleAssist(AssistState)` (API 29) gives `getAssistStructure()` view text (null when disabled, empty stub for secure windows) and `AssistContent` with the web URI in Chrome, once per visible activity in multi-window. API 37 adds `SHOW_WITH_ASSIST_STRUCTURE_SCREEN_CONTENT` (256). Screenshot goes to `/api/check/image`, text to `/api/devices/check`.
- Senior setup, 3-4 taps: 1) Settings > Apps > Default apps > Digital assistant app > Scam Shield, 2) confirm the "will be able to read information on your screen" dialog, 3) enable "Use screenshot" and "Use text from screen"; Pixel: System > Gestures > Press & hold power button = Digital assistant; Samsung: Advanced features > Side button > Long press > Digital assistant app, toggles "Analyze on-screen text" and "Analyze on-screen images", Bixby on the side button takes precedence. Deep link `Settings.ACTION_VOICE_INPUT_SETTINGS` (unverified). "Hey Google" and Gemini after the switch: unverified, likely lost.
- Precedents (unverified, press): ChatGPT on the default-assistant page since beta 1.2025.070 (2025-03-14; power button opens voice mode in a bubble; screen sharing via MediaProjection), Perplexity Assistant (2025-01-23, analyzes on-screen content), Microsoft Copilot (beta 27.9.420225014, 2024-02-26, no access to other apps). Play: "Default Assistant handler" is a permitted use of READ/RECEIVE/SEND/WRITE_SMS, RECEIVE_MMS, RECEIVE_WAP_PUSH and READ_CALL_LOG, subject to review; no assistant-specific policy found.
- Build, 2-3 days: day 1 service, session service, session with a Compose content view ("Checking what's on your screen..."), flatten `AssistStructure` text, hand-off to `VerdictActivity`; day 2 image upload, server prompt variant with package name, visible text and URL, French strings; day 3 device test (emulator `adb shell input keyevent KEYCODE_ASSIST` or long-press Home in 3-button navigation, unverified) and copy. Fallback: screenshot plus share.

Default SMS app (ROLE_SMS)
- Only the default SMS app receives `SMS_DELIVER_ACTION` and `WAP_PUSH_DELIVER_ACTION` and writes the SMS provider; it must ship a `SMS_DELIVER` receiver (BROADCAST_SMS), a `WAP_PUSH_DELIVER` receiver for `application/vnd.wap.mms-message` (BROADCAST_WAP_PUSH), an activity for `ACTION_SENDTO` with sms:, smsto:, mms:, mmsto:, and a `RESPOND_VIA_MESSAGE` service (SEND_RESPOND_VIA_MESSAGE). Requestable with `createRequestRoleIntent(ROLE_SMS)`; grants the phone, contacts, sms, storage, microphone, camera and notifications sets. Still no RCS: Google Messages alone has it, behind a hidden allowlist, no public API by Oct 2026.
- 7-10 working days, and it takes the senior out of Google Messages, which runs on-device Scam Detection on SMS, MMS and RCS from non-contacts (English first, US, UK, Canada, since 2025-03-04). Play: "Default SMS handler" row, declaration form, review time unknown; Fossify and Simple Mobile Tools references are GPL (unverified). Do not build.

Call screening (ROLE_CALL_SCREENING)
- `CallScreeningService` with `BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE` (signature|privileged); role via `createRequestRoleIntent(RoleManager.ROLE_CALL_SCREENING)` and `startActivityForResult`; one system dialog ("default caller ID & spam app"), not a restricted setting on 15/16. `onScreenCall()` fires for incoming and outgoing calls whose number is not in contacts; `Call.Details` gives `handle`, `callDirection`, `callerNumberVerificationStatus` (PASSED, FAILED, NOT_VERIFIED); `respondToCall` within 5 s or the phone rings; responses disallow, reject, silence, skip call log, skip notification; a caller-ID activity may be launched from `onScreenCall` (background-launch exemption on 14-16 unverified). The role grants the `notifications` set and the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW app-op. No audio: third-party recording blocked since Android 10, accessibility workaround banned on Play in May 2022. `TelecomManager.ACTION_POST_CALL` (API 30) carries EXTRA_HANDLE, EXTRA_DISCONNECT_CAUSE, EXTRA_CALL_DURATION (VERY_SHORT to LONG); its recipient is unverified (possibly dialer only).
- Build, 1 day: service, role request, plain full-screen caller-ID activity ("Unknown number. Not one of your people. Never share codes or buy gift cards."), a "stay alert" notification after the call; lookups local or a sub-second server check, never Muse inside the 5 s. Emulator simulates an incoming call. Fallback: none needed; the scene is optional.
- Context: Android 16 blocks, during a call with a non-contact, disabling Play Protect (Android 6+), first-time sideloading from a browser or messaging app, and granting accessibility; bank-app screen-share warnings piloted in the UK (Monzo, NatWest, Revolut) and the US (Chase, Cash App, unverified). Pixel Scam Detection for calls: on-device, Pixel 9+, US English, off by default, beeps during the call. Samsung One UI 8.5 adds AI call screening with a live transcript on S26 then S25 (unverified). Truecaller and Hiya ship the role.

Full-screen intent vs overlay
- `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` (normal level) is enabled by default for every app installed on Android 14+; only the Play Store revokes it at install for apps without calling or alarm functions, and third-party stores set the initial state through `PackageInstaller.SessionParams`. Check `NotificationManager.canUseFullScreenIntent()` (API 34); deep link `ACTION_MANAGE_APP_USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` (Settings > Special app access > Manage full screen intents). Play: declaration required since May 31, 2024; since Jan 22, 2025 only calling and alarm apps are default-enabled, others request it at runtime.
- Change, 0.5 day: add the permission; on `scam` verdicts call `setFullScreenIntent(pendingIntent, true)` on the existing IMPORTANCE_HIGH, CATEGORY_MESSAGE, VISIBILITY_PRIVATE builder; give `VerdictActivity` `android:showWhenLocked="true"` and `android:turnScreenOn="true"`; keep `publicVersion` (Android 15 hides notification content during screen share unless one is set; Developer Options "Disable screen share protections" exists for recording); show a one-time card to the settings page when `canUseFullScreenIntent()` is false. Heads-up while the phone is in use, red screen on a locked or idle phone. Verify after a file-based install (5 minutes).
- Overlay: `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` through `Settings.ACTION_MANAGE_OVERLAY_PERMISSION` and `canDrawOverlays()`; a restricted setting on sideloaded 15/16; free through the call-screening or assistant role. Android 16 target changes touch intent matching only, nothing on notifications or overlays. Skip.

Distribution
- Demo: `adb install` on the recording phone (no prompt, `pm grant` works), or film the "Allow restricted settings" tap as part of the relative's 2-minute setup. Android 16 also refuses a first-time sideload from a browser or messaging app during a call with a non-contact.
- Developer verification: enforced since Sep 30, 2026 only in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand (certified devices, Android 7+), global in 2027; limited-distribution accounts share with up to 20 devices without ID or fee; the power-user advanced flow has a 24-hour lock (unverified) and ADB stays open. US and French sideloads are untouched this quarter.
- Play internal testing: up to 100 testers, builds live within minutes, "might not be subject to standard Play policy or security reviews", exempt from the Data safety section; a first upload shows temporary listing data for up to 48 hours; closed testing up to 200 lists of 2,000, open testing unlimited or capped at 1,000 or more. Personal accounts created after Nov 13, 2023 need a closed test with 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days before "Apply for production"; review usually 7 days or less. Any Play build drops `RECEIVE_SMS` (the manifest entry alone violates the SMS policy) and keeps the listener, PROCESS_TEXT, the share target and optionally the assistant role. The team's account type and creation date are unknown.
- Risk: if Play Protect's fraud pilot reaches the US or France, a sideloaded APK declaring RECEIVE_SMS or asking notification access is blocked at install ("App blocked by Play Protect") and the Play track becomes mandatory.

## Corrections to feasibility.md

- #6: A sideloaded RECEIVE_SMS APK is not "fine off-store" on Android 15/16: the SMS permission group is a restricted setting for file or browser installs, so a relative taps "Allow restricted settings" once in App info first; `adb install` bypasses it.
- #7: The notification listener is the real third-party RCS route, shipped by Bitdefender and Truecaller; it costs one notification-access toggle (plus restricted settings on sideloaded 13+), and Android 15+ redacts any notification carrying an OTP.
- #8: Call screening also sees outgoing non-contact calls and STIR/SHAKEN status; the role grants the overlay app-op with one dialog, no restricted setting. Android 16 blocks sideloads and accessibility grants during non-contact calls; Pixel call Scam Detection is Pixel 9+ only.
- #13: Overlays are a restricted setting on sideloaded 15/16; full-screen intents are granted by default on 14+ sideloads (Play revokes them unless calling or alarm). Add `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` to `VerdictActivity` (0.5 day) instead of an overlay.
- #15: The image share target is 0.5-1 day as estimated; a text share target adds 1 hour and PROCESS_TEXT 2-4 hours on the same activity, with a VIEW/BROWSABLE https filter so Gmail shows it.
