# Data watch: sources, platforms, prior art, classification (research, 2026-11-04)

Every "works today" cell was tested on 2026-11-04 with curl (browser user agent) or WebFetch from Vlad's Mac. "Licence" is what the publisher states; "fair use" means no explicit reuse grant, so quote a headline and one sentence, link, never mirror. Unconfirmed items are marked (unverified).

## Findings in five lines

1. Breaches: Have I Been Pwned is the only free, structured, verified-flagged breach feed; add ransomware.live (claimed victims) and the California AG CSV (filings, no sizes). Maine's list is now behind a login after abuse; the HHS OCR portal and the ICO dataset are machine-hostile but public-domain or OGL.
2. New techniques: no FTC Consumer Alerts RSS exists (email only); the usable official feeds are IC3 PSA + CSA, CISA news, NCSC UK, Europol, cybermalveillance (already used). Press: Krebs, BleepingComputer, The Record, Malwarebytes Labs, Google Threat Intelligence, Securelist, Which? news.
3. Social: X (pay-per-use only, anti-scraping terms), LinkedIn (no read API, scraping banned, hiQ lost) and Reddit (RSS ends 2026-11-13, public API ends March 2027) are off limits. Bluesky (open AppView + Jetstream), Mastodon hashtag timelines and YouTube Data API (10k units/day) are fine. Telegram public channels are a grey zone.
4. Prior art classifies by editorial judgement, not schema: HIBP flags + PwnCount, AARP/FTC/Which? human alerts, Feedly TI ($19k+/yr), GASA survey reports. Nobody publishes an AI-ranked public "scam watch" for seniors.
5. Schemas to borrow: HIBP breach model + VERIS (data_disclosure, data.variety) + ENISA severity for breaches; MITRE ATT&CK T1566/T1598 + the Federal Reserve ScamClassifier (nine scam types) + UK Finance APP categories for techniques.

## 4. Prior art

- HIBP: "latest breaches" feed; importance = PwnCount + flags (IsVerified, IsFabricated, IsSpamList, IsMalware, IsStealerLog); no ranking, no AI.
- Krebs, BleepingComputer, The Record, Malwarebytes Labs: editorial; no taxonomy exposed. DataBreachToday and HIPAA Journal: breach-by-breach reporting with counts.
- FTC Consumer Alerts (email), AARP Watchdog Alerts (email/text, 2 per month), Which? scam alerts (rolling article + email): human-written, plain-language, no schema.
- Feedly for Threat Intelligence: "AI Feeds" (200 per plan) ranking by threat actor, malware, TTP; US$19,200/yr Standard, $38,400 Advanced (aggregator listings, unverified). Reddit cut Feedly's API access on 2026-11-03.
- SOCRadar LABS: free Phishing Radar and campaign pages; enterprise product otherwise.
- GASA: Global State of Scams 2025 with Feedzai (46,000 adults, 42 markets; shopping 54%, investment 48%, unexpected money 48%); no public taxonomy; points to the Fed ScamClassifier. ScamWatchHQ is a pages.dev blog plus a custom GPT, not an authority.
- MISP default feeds / OpenCTI: OpenPhish, PhishTank, URLhaus, abuse.ch are URL indicator lists in MISP/CSV/freetext, not technique narratives; no free STIX/TAXII feed about consumer scams found. Phishing Initiative (Orange Cyberdefense) is a reporting form, no public feed. APWG: quarterly Trends Report PDF (Q1 2026 latest; brand and sector counts); eCrime Exchange is members-only (US$5,000 to 7,500/yr).
- BBB Scam Tracker: crowd reports reviewed before publication, public search, partner API; bot-blocked. Ransomware.live: open API of leak-site claims.
- Nobody publishes an AI-read, senior-facing public watch with a verdict per item; the field ranks by hand.

## 5. Classification fields

Breach, "big or important" (borrowed from HIBP, VERIS, ENISA, legal thresholds):
- `verification`: VERIS `data_disclosure` = Yes (confirmed) / Potentially (at risk) / No / Unknown, with `source_kind` = regulator filing (OCR, CA AG) > company disclosure (HIBP DisclosureUrl) > HIBP verified load > press > leak-site claim (ransomware.live). HIBP flags IsFabricated, IsSpamList, IsMalware, IsStealerLog demote.
- `people_affected`: HIBP PwnCount or filing count; thresholds the field uses: HIPAA 500+, California 500+ residents, GDPR art. 34 "likely to result in a high risk".
- `data_classes`: HIBP's 166 classes mapped to VERIS `data.variety` (Credentials, Bank, Payment, Medical, Personal, Secrets, Internal, Other); weight credentials + SSN/IBAN/health/DOB above emails only.
- `dates`: BreachDate vs AddedDate (HIBP warns breaches surface long after); `population`: VERIS `victim.country` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), `victim.industry`, `employee_count` band; `severity`: ENISA 2013 score = data-processing context x ease of identification + circumstances.
- Rank: verified AND (people >= 1M OR high-weight class) AND senior's country match AND added in the last 30 days; the card then says the one action (change the password, watch the bank, expect impostor calls citing this breach).

Technique, "new way of scamming" (borrowed from ATT&CK, ScamClassifier, UK Finance, APWG):
- `channel`: ATT&CK T1566 .001 attachment / .002 link / .003 via service / .004 voice, and T1598 .001 to .004 (phishing for information); extend with SMS, WhatsApp, in-person, postal.
- `scam_type`: Federal Reserve ScamClassifier: confirm scam -> authorized vs unauthorized payment -> category (Products or Services / Relationship and Trust) -> nine types: Merchandise, Investment, Property Sale or Rental, Romance Impostor, Government Impostor, Bank Impostor, Business Impostor, Relative/Family/Friend, Other Trusted Party. UK Finance APP categories (purchase, investment, romance, advance fee, invoice and mandate, CEO, impersonation police/bank, impersonation other) are the UK equivalent.
- `impersonated` (APWG brand / sector, or agency / relative), `lure` (pretext), `ask` (gift card, crypto, wire, remote access, OTP, QR code), `first_seen`, `novelty` (new vs variant), `countries` and `languages`, `source` + `confidence`, `target` (seniors flag).
