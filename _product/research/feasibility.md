Technical feasibility questions for Scam Shield, with a first-pass answer each (written 2026-10-27, deadline Nov 4, judged from a 3-minute video).
A session picks an Open or Researched question, researches or prototypes it, then updates Status (Open / Researched / Decided) and Finding here, and the matching `_product/specs/` file if a decision follows.
Findings come from the code (see `_product/spec.md`) and the sources below; "(unverified)" marks claims not checked against an official page.

| 1 | What can the current Gmail OAuth sync do, and what can it never do? | Gmail | Decided | The video's core path; promises must match the code | Built on `gmail.modify`: list, get, history, labels, modify, watch; the client refuses send, trash, delete and the SPAM/TRASH labels, so Scam Shield labels and moves but never deletes or replies. Never run on a live inbox yet (consent screen still to configure); new-project quotas are 6,000 units/min/user and 1.2M/min/project, about 100 full `messages.get` per user per minute, enough for the 50-message backfill (~3,000 units). |
| 2 | Real-time arrival: Pub/Sub push vs polling, and what latency to promise? | Gmail | Researched | "Checked within seconds" only holds on the push path | A watch lasts at most 7 days and Google says renew daily (the worker renews every 12 h); the Pub/Sub message carries only the address and a historyId, then `history.list` (2 units). Arrival-to-verdict is the Pub/Sub pull cadence plus Muse (median ~4.7 s at low effort, ~11.5 s at default), so "under 15 s" is defensible on push and "about 2 minutes" on the 120 s poll. |

| 4 | A "Report / Check this" button inside Gmail: add-on or Chrome extension? | Gmail | Researched | A surface the judges recognise; the Report feature's entry point | A Gmail add-on renders a card in the side panel when a message is opened, installs unpublished on the developer's own account (Apps Script: Deploy, Test deployments, Install), and `gmail.addons.current.message.readonly` is only Sensitive, so no CASA. A hold is possible only with a Gmail filter that skips the inbox (`users.settings.filters`, Restricted `gmail.settings.basic`) and a release after the verdict, which risks stranding mail if the worker dies. Promise "checked before you open it in Scam Shield" instead. |
| 6 | Android SMS: is the companion's RECEIVE_SMS allowed, and what does Play require? | SMS | Researched | Sideload vs store, and the product story | Today: a sideloaded APK with RECEIVE_SMS only, fine off-store. On Play, RECEIVE_SMS is in the restricted SMS group: allowed for the default SMS handler, or via a temporary "text-message phishing (smishing) defense" exception that requires "a track record of significant protection for users" shown by independent analysis, which a new app cannot show. Share-sheet input is the policy-safe fallback. |
| 7 | RCS on Android and SMS on iOS: what can a third-party app see? | SMS | Researched | Limits the SMS story to Android SMS/MMS | RCS is reserved for Google Messages (no public read API; a notification listener is a fragile workaround (unverified)). The iOS path is the share sheet or a screenshot. |
| 8 | Phone calls: can we screen or listen to calls on Android or iOS? | SMS | Researched | Call scams are the senior-first headline, but access is narrow | Android `CallScreeningService` (role `ROLE_CALL_SCREENING`, Android 10+) gets the number of non-contacts before ringing and 5 s to reject, silence or skip the log; no audio. Third-party call recording is blocked since Android 10 and Play banned the accessibility workaround in May 2022; only the default dialer can analyse call audio (Google's own Scam Detection: on-device, US English, off by default). iOS offers Call Directory and Live Caller ID lists only. |
| 9 | Feeding a call or a doorstep pitch to the model: which route and what latency? | Voice | Researched | "Pressure coach" with live audio | Routes: (a) speakerphone plus the app microphone and streaming STT (Muse Voice Transcribe realtime WebSocket, $0.18/h, 25 languages, French unconfirmed; or Android SpeechRecognizer, which stops at pauses and must be re-armed); (b) a Twilio number conferenced into the call (Media Streams / ConversationRelay, vendor-claimed median under 0.5 s). Add Muse at low effort (~4-5 s): advice lands 5-8 s after a sentence, usable for "hang up now", not turn by turn. Notes #9. |
| 10 | Live listening: is it legal in France, the US and the EU? | Voice | Open | A coach that listens to a scammer still records a person | France, article 226-1: capturing, recording or transmitting private words without consent is an offence; consent is presumed when done openly ("au vu et au su") and the person could object, so announced, visible listening on the senior's side, nothing stored, is the defensible setup. US: one-party consent federally, all-party in about 11-12 states (CA, FL, WA, IL, MD, MA, PA...) and the caller's state can apply. Needs counsel before launch. Notes #10. |
| 11 | Voice-clone detection: feasible? | Voice | Researched | "Grandchild in trouble" calls | Vendors claim 94-98% (DetectifAI 94.5% with 8.4% false positives; Resemble "up to 98%") on their own benchmarks; no independent figure on 8 kHz telephone audio. Not a week's work, and a false alarm on a real grandchild is costly; the honest feature is detecting the ask (urgency, secrecy, money, codes, which the detector already scores on text) plus "hang up and call back on a number you already have". |
| 12 | Latency budget: model thinking vs the verdict promise | Real-time | Researched | Sets what the video can claim | Measured: low effort ~930 thinking tokens / 4.7 s, default ~2,950 / 11.5 s, minimal 2.8 s; corpus median 4.0 s; 20 s deadline, then the detector answers (`degraded`). ## Notes
- **#4** Add-on: Apps Script, contextual trigger on message open, cards only (no custom HTML), backend calls over HTTPS to Scam Shield (#25); public listing needs brand verification but the add-on scopes avoid CASA. Extension: the Scam Shield session cookie is same-site, so the extension would use a device token like the Android companion; InboxSDK needs an app id (unverified).
- **#9** Twilio route: the senior adds a Scam Shield number to the call (merge), audio streams to the server, STT then Muse; ~$0.014/min voice plus ~$0.07/min ConversationRelay (third-party figures). On-device route: Android 14+ needs a `microphone` foreground service type; a PWA can use `getUserMedia` only while in the foreground. Both routes end on Muse, so the floor is the model latency in #12.
- **#10** French law also bans transmitting words live (226-1 says "capter, enregistrer ou transmettre"), so a stream to a server is covered even without storage. Google ships its call Scam Detection on-device and in the US only, which is a hint about the EU risk. For the video: a doorstep scene where the senior says "my phone is listening" and the advice is spoken back, nothing stored.
- **#18** Honest week plan: a "Report" button that assembles a report pack (URL, sender, headers, screenshot, verdict) and opens the right channel with the text prefilled (Google form, 33700 or 7726 SMS, FTC form, Signal Spam, Gmail's own Report phishing), and sends automatically only to the registrar abuse address and to Netcraft once a key is confirmed. Never claim "reported to the authorities" for a form the senior must submit.
