## Decision
- **Build Listen first, with zero glasses code.** The phone's microphone hears both people (a visitor at the door, a pitch at a counter); Muse Voice Transcribe writes it down, Muse Spark judges it against her life, and the Android companion's existing TextToSpeech whispers two sentences through the glasses used as ordinary Bluetooth headphones. No Developer Mode, no approval, any earbuds work, so the Nov 1 cut cannot kill the scene. About 1.25 days (a device voice route plus a microphone foreground service with a 45-second buffer and a "What do they want?" tap).
- **Look second, on the DAT camera in Developer Mode**, only if a pair is in hand and the loop runs by Fri Oct 30: a photo of a letter or a big computer pop-up, verdict in the ear after the stream stops. Paper and large screens only; the glasses cannot read phone text (2-3 px x-height at 40 cm). Voice launch ("Hey Meta, start Prudence") last, and cut first: it needs a Developer Center Beta channel with no SLA.
- **The glasses' own microphones do not hear the other person.** The headset (HFP) microphone is beamformed to the wearer: someone facing her is "barely audible". The unbeamformed in-stream audio is beta-only, keeps the capture LED lit, drains the battery and mutes the glasses' speakers; on glasses release 129 opening HFP ends any DAT session. Listen with the phone, whisper with the glasses.
- **Meta's approval: not needed for the video, not possible for the public.** Developer Mode is five taps on "App version" in the Meta AI app and registers our APK on our own glasses in minutes; public publishing has no date, and the beta features stay unpublishable. Video and README wording: "Ray-Ban Meta as headphones" for Listen; "a demo build on Meta's Wearables Device Access Toolkit 1.0.1 in Developer Mode on our own glasses; glasses apps cannot be published yet" for Look; never "available on Ray-Ban Meta".
- **Legal setup.** Announced and visible: she turns it on and says so, the phone shows "Prudence is listening to help me. Nothing is kept.", nothing is stored, nobody is identified by face or voice, words only (no tone, which keeps it out of the AI Act's emotion-recognition definition). France art. 226-1 presumes consent when done openly; Massachusetts c. 272 §99 punishes only secret interception, and hearing without recording counts, so announcing is the defence, "nothing stored" is not. Every voice in the video is a consenting actor. Counsel before launch (feasibility #10, #59, #60).
- **Buy today in Paris: Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 Wayfarer, matte black, clear lenses, €449 at Boulanger** (30-minute click and collect at Haussmann Maison, Montparnasse or BHV; 15-day returns; per-store stock by phone, 09 69 32 32 23). Fallback the Gen 2 clear (€419, the model the docs name). Meta.com/fr would deliver after the cut (unverified). Gen 3 is supported since SDK 1.0.0 per Meta's reply on GitHub and appears to the SDK as `RAYBAN_META_OPTICS`, so the companion must never filter devices by model. Boston, only if Linda must wear the glasses on Hadrien's footage: Best Buy South Bay or Watertown ($449, unverified), or Ray-Ban and Sunglass Hut at the Prudential Center. No Display ($799 / €899, HUD to build, open microphone bugs), no Vanguard (sport lenses), no Ray-Ban Meta Audio (no camera, invisible to DAT).
- **The scene.** Linda at her front door taps Listen; a roofer (actor) wants "two thousand, cash, today only"; about nine seconds after her tap she hears "Do not pay cash today. Ask for a written quote."; the phone shows the Be careful card with "Leave me a written quote" big; narrator names Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Spark. A 5 s Look insert at the kitchen table if the hardware runs ("This letter is not from Medicare."). Candidate slot: a swap for 1:47-2:05, decided at the Nov 1 rehearsal. Fallback: the same take with one earbud, the letter through the phone camera (built).
- **Negotiation coaching stays out of the video.** Car dealer, rental deposit and contractor quote keep the scam frame (pressure, upfront payment, a new account) and widen the audience with no new product; salary, rent renewal and meetings make it a Cluely-style copilot that blurs "one verdict for a senior". One pitch line at most; a "Coach" mode after Nov 4.

## Corrections to earlier research and to the repo's assumptions
- `/api/devices/check` takes text only, so glasses photos and voice need a device-token route (meta-glasses-assistants.md assumed reuse); there is no bare `/api/check`.
- `extractFromImage` maps `letter` and `web` to `sms`, so a card about a photographed letter says "text message" today.
- Face to face, no second device is needed (feasibility #9 and #21 updated): the senior's own phone hears both people; the second device stays for calls on that phone.
- On the current glasses software the DAT camera session ends when the HFP microphone connects, on every model (Meta: a glasses update will fix it, no date): shoot the letter first, listen afterwards, never open SCO inside a session.
- Sunday Nov 1 (Toussaint): central Paris stores stay open (Boulanger lists 11-19 or 11-20); only parcels stop. Oct 30 is a Friday, not a Thursday.
