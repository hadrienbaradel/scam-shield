# Look closer (Muse Spark with tools)
Status: Built (2026-11-04: tested, run live on the Meta Model API and in a browser; no Claude Design pass yet, see Open questions) · Last updated: 2026-11-04

## Why
A verdict says what Prudence thinks; a frightened person still wonders "how do you know?". Under the card, "Look closer" has Muse Spark check the same message again with Prudence's own tools (how old the website is, where the link lands, the list of dangerous websites, the person's orders, companies and people, the known scams and breaches), and the card lists every check with its result in plain words, then a verdict whose every reason points to one of those checks. It is Meta's function calling doing product work, and an answer the person can verify line by line. It is a second look behind a button: the inbox path stays one call per message.

## Behavior
1. Where the app holds the whole message exactly as it was judged, the "Still unsure?" row shows a second button, "Look closer" ("Regarder de plus près"), next to "Ask <name>" (or "Add a trusted person"): the result of Check something (pasted words, the words read from a screenshot, a voice message's words, an email file) and the demo examples on `/#/demo`. The page sends back the message as the check judged it (every field: attachments, hidden text, a chat's earlier turns, an inbox's rows), and the look inspects its links again as the first check did, so its rules see what the first check's rules saw. On a Safe card it waits in the folded row behind "I still have a doubt", like Ask.
2. Tapping it shows "Prudence is looking closer. About ten seconds." ("Prudence regarde de plus près. Environ dix secondes.") under the row; the button cannot be tapped again while it runs.
3. Then the card lists "Prudence checked:" ("Prudence a vérifié :") with one line per check made, its result in plain words in the person's language, for example "The website usps-hold.test was registered 2 days ago (public registry)", "Where the link goes: shop.example, a page titled "Pay your fee" that asks for card details", "Not on the public list of dangerous websites" or "The list of dangerous websites was not checked (no key here)", "No USPS parcel in your orders; your parcels come by Amazon Logistics", "USPS is not one of the companies that write to you", "Matches the known scam "USPS package on hold, small redelivery fee"", "No known breach leaked your bank details".
4. Under the checks: "After looking closer: Scam" (Be careful, Safe; with the verdict's icon and colour, never colour alone), the headline, what to do, and one to three reasons, each ending with the check it rests on ("Check 2"). A reason that rests on no check made is not shown.
5. The last line names the model and the work: "Looked closer by Muse Spark (muse-spark-1.3): 4 checks in 7 s" ("Regardé de plus près par Muse Spark (muse-spark-1.3) : 4 vérifications en 7 s"); with another model selected, that model names itself.
6. Prudence checks only with what it already uses for every message, and only when the person taps: the public registry of website addresses (RDAP: how long ago the website was registered), where a link really lands and what its page asks for (opened with the guard of "where does this link really go?": no private addresses, no cookies, 5 redirects, 5 s, 256 KB, never a link that would unsubscribe or change something, and never for a message the first check already calls Scam; a landing page counts as clean only on the claimed company's own site or the authenticated sender's, never when it asks for a password or card details, and a page on a service where anyone publishes, such as sites.google.com or a free website builder, is a warning), the public list of dangerous websites (abuse.ch URLhaus, the host name only; "not checked" without its key), the person's recent orders and carriers, the companies that write to them, their trusted people (as a relation such as "your grandson", never a name, address or number), the known scam patterns, and the known breaches. On the demo examples, whose links use reserved addresses no registry knows, a link's age and landing page come from the example itself and the line ends "(example)".
7. Muse Spark may ask only about website addresses found in the message's links, or where a checked link leads; any other address, or arguments that do not fit the check, are refused and that call is not listed. At most 6 checks and 4 rounds with the model, 25 seconds for the whole look, 5 seconds for each lookup outside Prudence; the last round offers no checks at all, so Muse Spark has to answer.
8. A closer look never lowers a Scam. It may confirm the first verdict with new reasons, or raise it (Safe to Be careful or Scam, Be careful to Scam). It may lower Be careful to Safe only when at least two checks were made and every one came back clean (nothing found against the message and nothing left unknown) and the rules do not call the message a scam. The usual guardrails apply to its answer as to every verdict (a proven scam is never Safe, an authenticated sender the person knows is never Scam); when they would change it, or when nothing usable is left, there is no closer verdict: the card still lists the checks made, then says "Prudence could not look closer this time. The verdict above stands." ("Prudence n'a pas pu regarder de plus près cette fois. Le verdict ci-dessus reste valable.").
9. When the look cannot run (the model unreachable or too slow, too many looks in a minute), the card says "Prudence could not look closer right now. Please try again in a minute." with a Try again button, never the server's own words. Without a model that can use tools (no Meta key, or a provider without function calling such as Claude today) the button is not shown, and the server answers 503 `investigate_unavailable`.
10. Nothing new is stored: no check row, no model cache, no copy of the message or of the checks, on the server or the device (the trace lives on the screen until the person leaves the card). Nothing new is read, sent or reached either: the same registries, the same context and the same model as every other check, only when the person taps. So, under the settings-and-onboarding rule, Look closer has no Settings row, no welcome step and no protection-score criterion.
11. The inbox path does not change: Gmail sync and polling, Today's queue, WhatsApp, Messenger and the phone still make one model call per message; Look closer runs only on a tap.

## Out of scope
- My messages and the two-pane inbox, the old example list (`#/demo/all`), WhatsApp and Messenger replies, the Android companion: no Look closer (their cards are drawn elsewhere or have no room for it).
- Today's cards from the person's own Gmail: no Look closer. The sync judged each message with its Gmail thread and the page holds only the message (`/api/messages/:id`), so a look there would see less than the first check did.
- Meta's built-in web search (the real company's page as a check): Responses API only, $2.50 per 1,000 searches; not today.
- Claude as the model: its adapter refuses tools ("tools are not supported by this provider"); the other OpenAI-style presets get them but were not tried live.
- Keeping a closer look (in the check row, the audit list, the guardian's view): nothing is stored (line 10); "Ask <name>" shares the first verdict.

## Implementation pointers
- `lib/llm`: `complete({ tools })` and `toolCalls` in its answer; neutral `{ role: "assistant", content, toolCalls }` and `{ role: "tool", toolCallId, content }` messages (`openai-compatible.js`, used by `meta.js`); the mock scripts tool calls; `anthropic.js` refuses.
- `lib/investigate.js`: `TOOLS`, `INVESTIGATE_PROMPT`, `investigate()`, the plain-words results (EN, FR), the citation and verdict rules.
- `src/routes/investigate.js`: `POST /api/check/investigate` (signed in: `{ checkId, message, threadHistory }` from the check answer's `judged`, the check's own first verdict, the links inspected as `runCheck` does), `POST /api/demo/investigate` (an example by id), `investigateDeps()` (the real lookups); `src/routes/mail.js` answers `judged` on every `/api/check/*`.
- `public/js/views/card.js` (`unsureRowHtml({ lookCloser })`, `lookCloserHtml`, `bindVerdictCard({ onLookCloser })`, `runLookCloser`), `check.js`, `today.js`, `i18n.js` (`ck_look_closer`, `inv_*`), `styles.css` (`.vcard-checks`).

## Open questions
- No Claude Design pass today (`_product/docs/design.md` asks for one before a screen is built): the section reuses the card's own parts (section title, fact boxes, chips, the small source line); a design pass and a reading check with someone outside the team are owed before the screen is called final.
- Accuracy is not measured: no score of closer looks on the seed cases or the 500-email set, so it stays behind a button and off the inbox path. Cost: two model calls of about 5k tokens in and 1k out per look, on top of the first check.
- Should a closer look open a Scam's links when the person asks? Today never (line 6): a visit tells the sender the address is read, and the verdict cannot go lower anyway.
- Seen live: the model writes `"null"` as a word for an empty argument (read as no value), and once read "Prudence knows of no recent orders" as "you have no orders"; the facts and the prompt now say an empty context proves nothing, not yet re-measured beyond the three cases.
- With `ABUSECH_AUTH_KEY` unset (today), every look says the list of dangerous websites was not checked; the line is honest but repeats on every card.
