# Facebook and Messenger: send it to Prudence
Status: Partial (coded and tested; Meta's side set up on Nov 4 but for the App Secret; unverified on the live platform) · Raised by Vlad, 2026-11-04 (a stranger writing to him on Facebook right now) · Last updated: 2026-11-04

## Why
Most social-media scams start on Facebook ($2.1B lost in 2025 to scams that began on social media, Facebook first, FTC 2026; `research/social-scams.md`), and the costliest of them, the romance and investment arc ("pig butchering"), opens with a stranger's friendly message that asks for nothing for weeks. No API reads a personal Facebook, Snapchat or TikTok inbox, and Prudence reads inboxes through Meta's APIs only (never a browser session, never scraping: Vlad, Nov 4), so the senior shows Prudence the conversation: forward the messages, or a screenshot of the chat and of the profile, to the Prudence Page on Messenger, and the verdict comes back in the chat, judged on the whole conversation, with the stage it is at and what comes next. Second Meta surface (the Messenger Platform) carrying Muse Spark's verdict, after WhatsApp (`whatsapp-channel.md`), and Vlad's own live case is the demo.

## Behavior
1. **Send it to Prudence.** The senior opens the Prudence Page in Messenger once (the link in Settings, or a search for the Page) and sends it anything: a forwarded message (long-press, Forward, Prudence), the pasted words, a screenshot, a voice message. Within seconds one reply lands in the same chat: one verdict line (🔴 Scam, 🟠 Be careful, 🟢 Safe), the headline, up to three reasons, one next step, and who checked it ("Checked with Meta's Muse Spark"). The typing dots show while Prudence works.
2. **One conversation, one reply.** Several messages sent within a few seconds (a chat forwarded message by message, two screenshots) are judged together as one conversation and get one reply, never one per message; Prudence waits about three seconds after the last one.
3. **The stage and the next move.** A conversation is judged as a whole, never on its last message alone. When it follows a known scam path (a stranger's friendly opener then investment talk; a romance then money; a wrong number that keeps chatting; a job that pays to click), the reply says in plain words where it stands ("She wrote to you first, and now talks about what her trading mentor makes her") and what usually comes next ("Next she will ask you to put money on her app, then for a fee to take it out").
4. **The opener.** A stranger's first message that looks like a wrong number or an out-of-the-blue greeting with compliments or a photo ("Hi, is this Mary?", "Hello, I saw your profile") gets Be careful, never Safe, even though it asks for nothing: "Do not answer. This is how romance and investment scams start."
5. **Never, whatever else it says.** A message that asks to put money on a trading or crypto app, to pay a fee or a tax to take one's own money or earnings out, or to install an app from outside the app stores is a Scam.
6. **What a screenshot shows.** Muse Spark reads a picture for what it is, word for word and without judging on the way: one message; a chat (who said what, in order: "You" and the other person); or a profile page (what the platform itself shows: name, when the account was made, the country it is managed from, followers, verification, the job and place it claims). A list of conversations (a chat list, "Message requests", a spam folder) is read row by row (the name shown, the preview word for word, the date, the section it sits in) and judged as a list: each stranger who wrote first is named, the list is Be careful when there is one and Scam only when a preview itself shows a prize, a code, a money ask or an app link, and the next move is to reply to nothing, tap nothing and, to know more, open the chat and send Prudence a screenshot of it. The reply quotes only what is visible: a photo is never called fake from its look, and no face recognition is ever run.
7. **Voice and the rest, as on WhatsApp.** A voice message is written down by Muse Voice Transcribe and judged like a text; the reply quotes what it says (the first 200 characters). No words heard, no message in the picture, a sticker or a file, a picture over 8 MB or a recording over 16 MB each get one plain sentence.
8. **Language.** The reply is in French when the words are French, in English otherwise.
9. **Nothing kept.** Not the messages, the pictures, the recordings, nor the verdict; the sender is a Messenger id, not an account, so the check uses the shipped pattern cards and no personal context.
10. **Once, and not too often.** Each message is answered once however many times Meta delivers it, and Prudence's own replies echoed back are ignored; a sender gets at most 10 checks a minute, the eleventh gets "Please wait a minute before sending another message." and the rest of that minute is silence.
11. **Fail closed.** Meta's webhook is verified (the GET handshake with the verify token) and every delivery must be signed (`X-Hub-Signature-256` with the app secret): an unsigned or mis-signed delivery is refused, and so is every delivery while the app secret is not set. Without the Page token the server still starts and the webhook answers 501; the handshake works with the verify token alone. Pictures and recordings are fetched over https from Meta's own hosts only, with no token attached, never following a redirect.
12. **Degraded.** When Muse Spark is not switched on, a text is judged by the rules alone and the reply says so; a picture or a voice message gets "Checking pictures and voice notes needs Meta's Muse Spark. Please type the words instead." When something fails on our side: "Something went wrong on our side. To be safe, do not click anything in the message."
13. **Settings and welcome.** Settings has a row "Facebook and Messenger": with the Page configured, "Send any message to Prudence on Messenger" and the button "Open Messenger" (the m.me link; on a computer the same link as a QR code to scan with the phone); without it, the Coming soon row of `protection-menu.md`. The welcome has no step for it: the channel needs nothing from the person (no number to match); the "You are protected" screen lists it under "Add it any time in Settings".
14. **Who can use it today.** Before App Review, only people with a role on the Meta app (the team, up to 50 testers) get a reply; Meta drops everyone else's messages before they reach Prudence. The public needs App Review for `pages_messaging` and Business Verification, after the hackathon (`public-deployment.md`).
15. **The same judgment in the app.** Words pasted in "Check something" that hold several turns ("Daniel: … / Me: …") and a screenshot of a chat sent there get the conversation judgment of lines 3 to 6, with the senior's life context when signed in ("She is not one of your trusted people").

## Demo path
Opening shot: Vlad's own "Message requests" screenshot sent to Prudence, answered 🟠 Be careful with the stranger who wrote first named. Then Vlad's phone, Messenger open on the stranger who wrote first ("Hi handsome, I saw your profile", a photo, three weeks of small talk, then "my mentor's platform made me $8,400 this month, start with $500"). He long-presses her last messages, taps Forward, picks Prudence; the typing dots; one reply: "🔴 Scam. This is the 'new friend, then investment' trick: she wrote to you first, moved the talk to private messages and now shows you her profits. Next she will ask you to put money on her app, then for a fee to take it out. Send nothing. Show this to someone you trust. Checked with Meta's Muse Spark." Narrator: "Meta's Muse Spark reads the whole conversation, not one message, and sees where it is going before the money moves." Optional 5 s: a screenshot of her profile ("Joined August 2026 · Based in Nigeria · 12 friends") read by Muse Spark, a red reply quoting those facts. Needs the Page, a role on the app for Vlad's account, the tunnel; fallback: the same chat pasted in Check something at phone width (line 15).

## Out of scope
Reading anyone's Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat or TikTok inbox (no API exists for personal accounts; a professional Instagram account's DMs are the next step, Idea "Connect Instagram"); the Android notification listener (its own Idea); a public Page (App Review, Business Verification); matching the sender to a Prudence account; "Ask my trusted person" from the chat; group chats; Marketplace-specific checks; the hiring-file, code-repository and investment-platform checks of `social-scams.md`; reporting the account to Meta (`report-upstream.md`).
