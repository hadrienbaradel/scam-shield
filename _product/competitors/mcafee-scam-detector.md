# McAfee Scam Detector

Category: security vendor, bundled AI scam detection.

## What it does
- Announced at CES (Jan 2025), launched May 14, 2025, included in all McAfee core plans at no added cost (McAfee+ list prices not verified). Won "Best Use of AI in Cybersecurity" at the 2025 A.I. Awards (McAfee press release).
- Texts: Android scans automatically and warns before you open; iPhone screens suspicious texts or checks on demand. Email: connects Gmail, Microsoft and Yahoo accounts and flags scam mail. Video: on-device deepfake detection on YouTube, TikTok and others (96% claimed). On-demand checks of uploaded messages, screenshots and links; works in iMessage, WhatsApp and browsers. Alerts explain why a message was flagged "so users learn to spot scams themselves". Claim: text scam detection "exceeds 99% accuracy" (vendor, no independent test).

For whom: consumers and families. Channels: SMS, email, video, web. Detection: ML on the full text plus McAfee reputation; on-device for video. Install: McAfee app with email accounts connected. Price: bundled in paid plans.

## Strengths
- Automatic email scanning across providers (the closest to our Gmail watch); explanations; multi-channel; big brand.

## Weaknesses
- Needs the McAfee suite; no personal context (bank, carrier, orders, relatives); no guardian; explanations are generic ("urgency, unknown sender"); flags but does not act in the inbox; accuracy self-reported.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Connecting an email account for scanning is accepted consumer practice; useful for the Google verification argument.
- Our labels, move and "Put it back" go beyond flagging; state the no send/trash/delete rule as the safety counterpart.
