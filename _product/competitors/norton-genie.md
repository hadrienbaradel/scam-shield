# Norton Genie and Norton Scam Protection (Gen Digital)

Category: security vendor, free checker plus bundled suite features.

## What it does
- Genie app (July 2023; free; iOS, Android, web): upload a screenshot or paste text and links; instant verdict; follow-up questions ("why is it flagged?", "how do I avoid this?"); trained on "millions of scam samples" plus Norton data. English-only analysis.
- Norton Scam Protection (English launch Feb 2025; global Nov 3, 2025) included at no extra cost in Norton AntiVirus Plus, Mobile Security and Norton 360: Safe SMS (Genie AI reads the meaning of texts; mobile only), Safe Web, Genie Assistant, Deepfake Protection (English YouTube/Facebook audio; automatic only on Copilot+ PCs). Scam Protection Pro on Norton 360 Advanced adds Safe Email (hidden scam patterns before opening) and Safe Call (labels or blocks scam calls). Gen Threat Labs: social engineering was more than 80% of threats observed in 2025, 110 attempts blocked per second.
- US first-year list prices (promo pages, renewal higher): Norton 360 Standard $94.99, Deluxe $119.99, Select Plus with LifeLock $189.99. The Genie app is free.

For whom: consumers; LifeLock bundles for identity theft; no senior edition. Channels: SMS, web, email (Pro), calls (Pro), video. Detection: LLM/ML on message meaning plus Norton reputation. Install: app or suite; Safe SMS is an iOS filter extension or Android integration. Price: free app, $95-190 a year for suites.

## Strengths
- Brand trust with older buyers; follow-up Q&A on a verdict is real coaching; cross-channel in one suite; deepfake audio detection, which we do not have.

## Weaknesses
- Email protection only in the top tier; verdicts generic (no knowledge of the user's bank or orders); pricing and upsell complexity; no guardian loop; English-only analysis.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Make "ask more" visible: our read-aloud reasons answer "why?"; the demo should show a follow-up tap.
- Deepfake voice is out of our scope; say so rather than imply it.
