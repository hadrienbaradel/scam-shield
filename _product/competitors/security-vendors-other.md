# Security vendors with AI scam checkers (grouped)

Every large consumer-security brand added a free "paste it here" checker between 2023 and 2026. None knows the user; none has a caregiver loop; most explanations are one line.

- Malwarebytes Scam Guard (June 3, 2025): AI chat advisor inside Malwarebytes Mobile Security (iOS, Android), free and paid tiers alike. Inputs: texts, DMs, emails, images, links. Covers romance, phishing, delivery and robocall scams; user reports feed its learning. Price: free in the app. https://www.malwarebytes.com/press/2025/06/03/malwarebytes-launches-scam-guard-an-ai-powered-mobile-first-digital-safety-companion-to-combat-todays-most-pernicious-threats
- Avast Scam Guardian (June 25, 2025; Gen Digital): free in Avast Free Antivirus, checks websites, messages and emails with no setup; Scam Guardian Pro in Avast Premium Security adds AI email scam screening. Mobile (Nov 12, 2025): basic free in Avast Mobile Security; Pro in Premium Mobile Security with call screening and email analysis. Pro price not found. https://newsroom.gendigital.com/2025-06-25-Avast-Makes-AI-Driven-Scam-Defense-Available-for-Free-Worldwide
- Aura: identity and financial protection; AI Call Assistant for scam and unwanted calls (family plans); Individual $12/mo, Couple $22/mo, Family $32/mo billed annually (TechRadar, MoneyLion Apr 2026); 24/7 US support. No senior product, no message checker found. https://aura.com/pricing
- LifeLock: sold as Norton 360 with LifeLock (Select Plus $189.99 first year) with Genie Scam Protection Pro; see norton-genie.md.
- F-Secure: free Text Message Checker (beta web form, AI verdict, identifying details stripped within a month); F-Secure Total SMS protection (iOS, Android; AI moves harmful texts to junk); standalone Scam Protection app on a monthly plan with a 7-day trial (price not found). https://www.f-secure.com/en/text-message-checker
- Guardio: Chrome and Edge extension plus mobile; AI blocks scam sites, smishing and malicious notifications; breach monitoring. Free tier (basic, one user); Family (5 users) $34.99/mo or $279.90/yr; VIP add-on $199.99/yr; no free trial, 3-day refund window; many complaints about surprise charges (reported by Aura, a competitor). https://www.security.org/antivirus/guardio/
- Trend Micro ScamCheck: free app with in-app purchases ($1.99-$59.99; 30-day trial): AI check of numbers, links, emails, messages and images; AI Video Scan for face-swap video calls; call and text blocking; US, Canada, Japan, Italy, Taiwan. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tm-check-spam-call-blocker/id1566099565
- Surfshark Email scam checker: Chrome extension widget in Gmail, runs only on click, an unnamed open-source LLM; needs a Surfshark One plan. https://surfshark.com/antivirus-features/email-scam-checker
- Bitdefender Scam Copilot: see bitdefender-scamio.md.

For whom: general consumers. Channels: paste, screenshot, SMS, web; email only in paid tiers. Detection: LLM or ML plus vendor reputation. Install: app or suite. Price: free entry, $5-35/mo for suites and family plans.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Free entry is now expected; our free and open stance only differentiates with context, senior UX and the guardian loop.
- Guardio's refund complaints are a warning: seniors are the main victims of security-software upsell; keep pricing simple or absent.
- Trend Micro and Norton treat deepfake video as a feature; we should scope calls and video out explicitly.
