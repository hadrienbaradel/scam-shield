# Carefull

Category: senior-focused financial safety (money, not messages). The closest product to "judge against this person's normal life".

## What it does
- Links bank, card and investment accounts through Plaid (12,000+ institutions, unlimited accounts). AI monitors for 50+ aging-related issues: duplicate or recurring charges, suspicious fees, scam-linked withdrawals, accounts that suddenly become active, compared with the person's own typical spending. Adds dark-web, credit and home-title monitoring, up to $1M identity theft insurance per adult, password vault, LegacyKit (documents and contacts for relatives). Cannot move money.
- ScamCheck: assesses texts, emails and letters, rates the risk, explains the warning signs and next steps (inclusion in the paid plan not stated).
- Trusted Contacts: read-only access to alerts; the member decides who sees what. A membership covers a second adult (spouse or parent).

For whom: adults 55+ and caregivers; banks, credit unions and wealth firms (Carefull Pro, B2B2C; Nationwide, MassMutual, Cooperative Bank). Series A of $16.5M. Channels: bank transactions first; email, SMS and paper mail on demand. Detection: behavioural anomaly ML plus rules; ScamCheck AI. Install: iOS and Android app plus Plaid linking. Price: $29.99/mo monthly; annual billing "saves 17%" (about $299/yr per earlier coverage); 30-day trial; often free through a partner bank.

## Strengths
- Knows the person's normal money behaviour; consent-based family loop; bank distribution; insurance and live US support.

## Weaknesses
- Acts after money moves; expensive for direct consumers; bank linking is a trust hurdle for seniors; message checking is a side feature; no inbox watch.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Trusted Contacts (read-only, member-controlled) is our guardian model, already accepted by banks; cite it as a proven pattern.
- "Compared with this person's typical behaviour" is our pitch applied to money; we apply it to messages, upstream of the transaction.
- Banks pay for fewer scam losses: a bank or credit-union channel is a credible go-to-market after the hackathon.
