# Call and SMS blockers: Truecaller, Hiya, Nomorobo, RoboKiller

Category: number-reputation apps. They own the call channel; built-ins (Pixel, iOS 26, carriers) are eating their free tiers.

- Truecaller: caller ID and spam blocking for calls and SMS; Premium about $9.99/mo or $29.99-$74.99/yr (store listings conflict by country). AI Call Scanner (2024, detects AI voices), AI Message IDs (2025), Scam Checker (late Sept 2026; web and Android; check links, numbers and messages; India first; fed by the ScamFeed community, about 20,000 live reports; screenshot upload planned). Truecaller screened 12.9B messages and tagged 20.3M as scams in the week of Sept 14-20, 2026.
- Hiya: carrier-grade protection (BT landlines; 20M+ attempts blocked in four months in the UK) and the consumer Hiya AI Phone (Jan 2025; iOS, Android): AI screens calls live, deepfake voice detection, $9.99/mo after a 14-day trial; a free Chrome deepfake add-on. Hiya survey: about one in three Americans got an AI scam call in 2024; 34% of those lost money, $7,200 on average (vendor survey).
- Nomorobo: free on VoIP landlines; mobile Max $4.99/mo or $59.99/yr; family (4 devices) $79.99/yr; spam-text filtering (iOS), call screening, allow and block lists; stays out of call records.
- RoboKiller: about $4.99/mo or $40/yr; audio fingerprinting and predictive analysis; Answer Bots; SMS fraud protection; sits in the call path (privacy trade-off); known to mislabel legitimate calls.

For whom: general public; Hiya and Truecaller also sell to carriers. Channels: calls first, SMS second. Detection: number reputation, crowd reports, audio and voice ML; little understanding of text content beyond spam. Install: app set as default caller ID or SMS filter; carrier integration. Price: free to $9.99/mo.

## Strengths
- Stop the call before it rings; large reputation graphs; live AI call screening is the new frontier.

## Weaknesses
- Reputation fails on spoofed and fresh numbers; no email; no personal context; no explanation of the scam story; mislabelled doctor or pharmacy calls are a real cost for seniors.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Calls are the costliest channel for the 80+ group (FTC); do not pretend to cover them, recommend the built-in or carrier screener during onboarding.
- Truecaller's Scam Checker shows that "paste a link, number or message" web checks are becoming a commodity.
- ScamFeed (community reports) is the crowd version of our IC3 and cybermalveillance threat feed.
