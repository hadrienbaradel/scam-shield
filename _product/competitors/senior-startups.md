# Senior-focused scam startups (2025-2026)

Category: new entrants selling to adult children, senior housing and banks. None watches an inbox; none learns the person's providers or orders.

- SeniorShield (SeniorShield Inc., "cybersecurity experts and gerontologists"): app and web; upload screenshots or photos, or paste the text of emails and texts; AI verdict plus explanation and next steps; residents without the app text a screenshot instead; video and quiz lessons. Pilot at 2Life Communities (Boston) with USC: 102 residents trained one-on-one; a survey of 78 found 60% had been scammed online; 682 verification requests, 204 flagged as scams, 184 suspicious; 81% satisfied; 75% want to keep using it (LeadingAge CAST case study, Sept 9, 2026). Price not stated. The closest product to our checker, in a senior-housing channel.
- Scammer Guardian (Ohio LLC founded 2025; iOS and Android early 2026): a GPT-4o voice agent answers unknown calls, asks who is calling, connects or blocks within seconds; the guardian (adult child) gets an SMS when a scam call is blocked; from $29/mo after a 7-day trial; self-funded; seeking seed from AgeTech investors (names AARP Innovation Labs, Primetime Partners). Calls only.
- SeniorSafe (Sentien Corp., Stamford CT; June 25, 2026): cloud AI monitors live inbound and outbound calls on smartphones, feature phones, landlines and VoIP, intervening with AI plus humans; claims "99% of scam and fraud calls" stopped (vendor); carrier integrations planned Q3 2026; price "affordable" (not listed); the Android app must become the default phone app.
- Jortty (West Palm Beach; launched Jan 7, 2026): AI "tech concierge" for seniors; forward emails to scam@jortty.com or send text screenshots; claims 95% accuracy; free tier with capped checks; $19.99/mo for unlimited checks plus 24/7 tech help.
- Shield Seniors (Tejasvi Manoj, 17, Dallas; TIME Kid of the Year 2025): website and app with a learning section, screenshot chatbot, AI analyser and reporting links; demo stage in 2025 (availability unverified).

For whom: adult children (35-55) buying for a parent (65+), senior living, banks. Channels: calls (Scammer Guardian, SeniorSafe); email and SMS screenshots (SeniorShield, Jortty, Shield Seniors). Detection: LLM on pasted content or a voice agent. Install: app, sometimes replacing the phone app. Price: $0-$29/mo.

## Strengths
- Senior-first UX and distribution (senior housing, caregivers); human support (Jortty); pilot evidence that seniors use a checker when trained (2Life: 682 checks).

## Weaknesses
- "Bring me the message" or call-only; nothing automatic in the inbox; no personal context; accuracy claims unverified; $20-30/mo is heavy on a pension.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Quote the 2Life numbers as a demand signal in the pitch.
- The adult child is the buyer: show the guardian dashboard, not only the senior's screen.
- "Text a screenshot to a number" is a zero-install path worth a backlog line.
- Be explicit that calls are out of scope and point to built-in screeners.
