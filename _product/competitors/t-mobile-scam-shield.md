# T-Mobile Scam Shield, the naming conflict, and carrier built-ins

Category: carrier built-in (calls). Also the holder of our product name.

## T-Mobile Scam Shield
- Launched July 16, 2020 as an "Un-carrier" move. Free for T-Mobile, Metro and Sprint lines: Scam ID ("Scam Likely" label), Scam Block (network-level; dial #662#), Caller ID, scam reporting, allow list. Controls now live in the T-Life app; Metro keeps a "T-Mobile Scam Shield" app (App Store id1367276365; Android package published by T-Mobile USA).
- Scam Shield Premium: $4 per line per month (Category Manager, reverse number lookup, voicemail-to-text); included on Experience More and Experience Beyond plans.
- Calls only; SMS filtering is not documented. T-Mobile reports 41.5B calls flagged or blocked in 2022 and 19.8B in 2023. User complaints: scam calls still get through, legitimate businesses blocked, T-Life app bugs.

## Trademark and confusion risk
- T-Mobile's benefits page is titled "T-Mobile® ScamShield App" and the app page reportedly shows ® after ScamShield (search snippet). The app Terms of Use assign proprietary rights to T-Mobile and First Orion Corp. (PrivacyStar). The USPTO record itself was not retrieved (uspto.gov, Justia and uspto.report blocked automated fetches): treat it as a registered or at least heavily used US mark since 2020 for anti-scam telecom software (unverified).
- Second exact-name holder: Singapore's government "ScamShield" app (Open Government Products, Singapore Police Force, NCPC; launched Nov 20, 2020): blocks calls against a police blocklist, filters SMS from unknown senders on-device, WhatsApp bot to check messages (scamshield.gov.sg).
- Severity: HIGH for anything public in the US. Same name, same category, same channel (SMS and calls), a carrier with roughly 130M customers (unverified), plus a foreign government app. Practical effects: no SEO (every result is T-Mobile), app-store rejection or takedown risk, a likely cease-and-desist, and judge confusion in a 3-minute video ("isn't that T-Mobile's?"). For the hackathon entry: acceptable only as a declared working title; for launch: rename.

## Five alternative names (not cleared; run USPTO, EUIPO and INPI searches before use)
1. Second Opinion: what the product gives a frightened person at 9pm, and what the guardian loop is.
2. Lantern: shows the way; reads the same in French (lanterne); short and warm.
3. Kinsafe: family plus safety; says the caregiver loop in one word.
4. Trustline: the trusted person's line; "line" lands with seniors.
5. Inbox Guardian: descriptive, says email first and names the guardian role; weaker as a brand.

## Other carriers and OEMs (calls first)
- AT&T ActiveArmor: free (spam and fraud call blocking, unknown callers to voicemail, breach alerts; claims protection for suspicious texts and links); Advanced $7 per line per month since Feb 18, 2026 (was $3.99), free on Unlimited Premium and Extra. Reviews: uneven blocking.
- Verizon Call Filter: free tier; Plus was $2.99/mo in 2019 (current price unverified).
- Samsung Galaxy S26: Google's Gemini Nano call Scam Detection inside Samsung's Phone app (Feb 2026, US English).

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Carriers own the call channel for free. Position the product as the email, text and family layer above carrier call blocking, and point to the user's carrier blocker in onboarding.
