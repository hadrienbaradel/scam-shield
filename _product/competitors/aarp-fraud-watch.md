# AARP Fraud Watch Network

Category: nonprofit service; a partner more than a competitor.

## What it does
- Free for members and non-members: Helpline 877-908-3360 (weekdays 8am-8pm ET; prevention tips, next steps after fraud, referrals, language help); Scam-Tracking Map (aarp.org/scammap; user-submitted, not verified by AARP); Watchdog Alerts by email or text; free confidential Fraud Victim Support groups; education content.
- AARP sits in the National Elder Fraud Coordination Center with Meta, Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Walmart and Capital One. AARP Innovation Labs is the investor senior-safety startups name first.

For whom: 50+ and families. Channels: phone helpline, email and SMS alerts, web. Detection: human experts and community reports; no automated checker. Install: none. Price: free.

## Strengths
- The brand seniors trust; a human on the line; emotional support; policy reach.

## Weaknesses
- Not real-time; the senior must decide to call; no inbox or SMS integration; no personal context.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- When no guardian answers, offer the AARP helpline number as the fallback in the "I'll call you" path.
- Watchdog Alert wording is a model for our "scam of the week" cards.
- AARP's 2025 report summary is the cleanest public statement of the FBI and FTC numbers.
