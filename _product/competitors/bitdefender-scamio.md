# Bitdefender Scamio (and Scam Copilot)

Category: security vendor, free AI scam checker.

## What it does
- Scamio: free AI chatbot. Paste text, upload screenshots or photos of texts, emails and chats, send links or QR codes, or describe a situation in words; verdict in seconds plus tips (delete, block the sender, contact the real organisation). Matches content against predefined rules and Bitdefender's base of documented scam and phishing cases (sender details, layout, wording). Labelled "early access". Headline claim "No scam can avoid detection"; no published accuracy. German-language version launched (it-daily).
- Scam Copilot (Oct 2024): the paid layer inside Bitdefender Premium and Ultimate Security (individual and family): web, email (Gmail, Outlook), SMS on iOS and Android (conversation patterns), chat apps (WhatsApp, Messenger), regional Scam Wave Alerts, chatbot. Premium Security was about $49.99 for the first year in a 2025 review (unverified current price). Bitdefender's 2026 blog lists newer tools, Scam Radar and RealCheck (not researched).

For whom: general consumers. Channels: web chat, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Discord (after linking a Bitdefender account); suites add email and SMS. Detection: rules plus case base plus AI chat. Install: none for Scamio (chat), but a Bitdefender account is required; suites install an app. Price: Scamio free; suites paid.

## Strengths
- Free, no install, multi-input (image, QR), available inside the apps seniors already chat in; large threat intelligence behind it.

## Weaknesses
- The user must know to ask; the verdict is generic (no knowledge of the user's accounts or orders); account sign-up friction; a typing chatbot is not senior-first; no family loop; no automatic inbox watch outside the paid suites.

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Screenshot and QR input are table stakes (we have screenshots; QR via SAM 3.1 is in the PRD backlog).
- "Describe the situation in words" is a good fallback for a senior who cannot screenshot.
- WhatsApp as a delivery channel for checks and guardian answers is worth a backlog line.
