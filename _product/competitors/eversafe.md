# EverSafe

Category: senior-focused financial monitoring with trusted advocates.

## What it does
- Monitors banking, investment and retirement accounts, credit cards, credit files and real estate. Builds a profile of each member's past behaviour and flags erratic activity; alerts; 24/7 expert help; identity recovery support.
- Trusted Advocates: the member picks relatives, friends or professionals; adding them is free; the member sets what each one sees; advocates get view-only access and are notified about unusual transactions.

For whom: older adults, families, financial advisors (UBS advisor flyer), senior living (A Place for Mom page). Channels: financial accounts only; no email, SMS or call checking. Detection: behavioural profile plus rules. Install: web and app plus account linking. Price: about $6.36-$21.24/mo; Essentials $7.49/mo (Retirement Living review, affiliate, undated); 30-day trial; up to 40% off annual plans.

## Strengths
- Cheap entry tier; advocate model with granular consent; recovery support; advisor distribution.

## Weaknesses
- Sees money only after it moves; no message channel; no explanation of the scam itself; interface and marketing look dated (unverified).

## What Scam Shield can learn
- Wording: "your advocate sees only what you allow".
- Alerting the advocate without blocking the member keeps the senior in control, the same principle as "Ask my trusted person".
