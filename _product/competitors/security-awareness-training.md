## Senior-facing education programs
AARP Fraud Watch Network (main entry: [aarp-fraud-watch.md](aarp-fraud-watch.md)) publishes 8-question multiple-choice quizzes (holiday scams, investment fraud, identity theft) that show "CORRECT" or "INCORRECT" and a short explanation after each answer, then a score. Scam Spotter (Google and the Cybercrime Support Network, 2020) reduces the advice to three golden rules, "Slow it down", "Spot check", "Stop! Don't send", with a quiz meant to be taken with a senior. Take Five (UK Finance) teaches "Stop, Challenge and Protect" and offers a 9-scenario yes/no quiz, "Are you scam-savvy?". Cyber-Seniors trains young volunteers to teach seniors in webinars, including one on the grandparent scam (unverified). FTC Pass It On treats older adults as "part of the solution to the problem, not just the victims", with bookmarks, fact sheets, presentations and videos. In France, cybermalveillance.gouv.fr offers SensCyber, a three-unit e-learning course (about 25-45 minutes each, quizzes with a 60% pass mark, a certificate) open to the public since 2024, with no senior track (unverified). All are free, all use realistic examples, a short rule set and an explanation after each answer; none uses points or leaderboards, repeats a rule over time, or builds a lesson from the learner's own messages.

## Takeaways for Prudence
1. The vendors' unit is right for us: one story, one question, instant feedback, one rule, a few minutes, in a place the learner already is. Ours is the verdict screen and the Learn tab.
2. Riot and Elba personalise with real data (the learner's breaches, the real CEO's name); Prudence can go further with the senior's own flagged message and real providers.
3. Their gamification (scores, leaderboards, streaks) is built for employees and their managers; for 70+ it reads as pressure. Keep progress, a wall of learned rules, spaced review and sharing with the trusted person.
4. Nobody gives seniors short, repeated, personal lessons: senior programs are one-off quizzes, print and talks; vendors sell to employers. The gap is open.
5. Independent studies find little effect from employee training (UC San Diego Health 2025, ETH Zurich 2022): keep claims modest and let the verdict do the protecting.
