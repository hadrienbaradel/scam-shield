You are redesigning the phone screens of Prudence, a scam-protection app for people over 70 and the family member who helps them. Prudence reads the senior's Gmail (and, through a paired Android app, their texts), judges every message against the senior's own life with Meta's Muse Spark, and answers in plain words: Safe, Be careful or Scam, with one next step. The senior can also check anything else (a screenshot, a pasted text, a voice message) and ask a trusted person for a second opinion. Attached: the eight current screens at 390 px (01 Today, 02 a verdict card, 03 My email, 04 Check something, 05 Learn, 06 a lesson, 07 My trusted people, 08 Settings). Keep the brand (navy #172A45-ish header, gold #E8A317-ish accent, the round gold check mark, light grey page, white cards) and the typeface; redesign the structure and the screens.

The person: a frightened 70-year-old at 9 pm on a phone, often with the largest text size. Rules that do not bend: text 19 px or larger (design at 22 px body, and show one frame at the largest size); tap targets 64 px; one verdict and one action per screen, the next step is one button with a verb on it; plain words, never jargon ("website address", not "domain"; no percentages, no acronyms); red, amber and green always come with the word and an icon, never color alone; WCAG AA everywhere, AAA on the verdict; calm: no countdown, no blinking, no stacked dialogs; every action has its undo on the same screen; English and French with the same layout (French runs 20 percent longer). Light mode only.

What to design, as phone frames at 390 × 844:

1. The menu. Today the top of every screen is a wall of links (see 03) and the header stacks "Linda / Settings / Sign out" (01). Give the app one calm structure: four tabs at the bottom (Today, Check, Learn, My people, with icons and words), a Settings button (gear and the word) at the top right, Sign out as the last button of Settings. "My email" disappears from the menu: the message list opens from Today and from the email row of the list below. Show the header and the tab bar at Normal and at Largest text size; nothing wraps, nothing is cut.

2. "What Prudence protects": a list on Today under the greeting, and again as the first section of Settings. One row per channel, in this order, each with an icon, the name, its state in words and at most one button with a verb:
   - Your email: "On since Nov 3 · checked 2 minutes ago" → See my messages
   - Your text messages: "Needs the Prudence app on your Android phone" → Add my phone (or, when a phone is paired: "On, on Linda's phone" → Change)
   - Voice messages: "Send one any time" → Check a voice message
   - WhatsApp messages: Coming soon ("Forward a message to Prudence and the answer comes back in the chat")
   - Facebook and Messenger: Coming soon ("A message or a Marketplace chat that feels wrong")
   - Your phone calls: Coming soon ("A warning before you call back a number from a scam message")
   A "Coming soon" row is grey, has no button and one line of what it will do; it must never look tappable. Design Today with this list under the "1 message needs you" card, and the empty Today ("Nothing in your email needs you") with the three Check choices of frame 3 as the next thing to do.

3. Check something, rebuilt around three big choices before any form: "Take a screenshot and send it" (first and biggest: on a phone it opens the photos or the camera), "Paste the words", "Send a voice message"; "Or an email file" goes behind a small "More ways" link. Each choice opens its own one-question screen (the picture picker; the paste box with "Who sent it?" and "Where did you get it?" as two big chips rows, not a dropdown; the voice file picker). Show the three-choice screen, the paste screen and the screenshot flow (choose → "Reading your picture…" → the verdict card).

4. The verdict card after a check, with the "still unsure" row: keep frame 02's card (Be careful, headline, reasons, Listen) and under it two buttons, "Ask Thomas" (gold) and "Send it to Prudence" (navy outline) with the line "A person at Prudence, or a deeper automatic check, looks at it and answers on Today within a day." While it is not built the second button carries a small "Coming soon" tag and is not tappable; design both states. On a Safe card the row is folded behind "I still have a doubt".

5. Learn at zero: today the progress card reads "0 of 8 lessons · 0 stars" (05) and the game is invisible until the first lesson. Show the stars from the start: three empty drawn stars on every lesson card (the font has no ★; draw them), a badge row with the eight badges to earn in grey with the character emoji, and "Your first star is one minute away" in place of the zero line. No timers, no leaderboards, no levels, nothing that goes down. Keep the lesson screen (06) as it is, with the stepper and the drawn stars.

6. Settings: the "What Prudence protects" list first, then Your trusted person, When a message is a scam (today's radio pair, frame 08), Tell Thomas automatically, Text size, Language, Read aloud, What Prudence knows about you (→ Look), What Prudence did (→ Look), What Prudence keeps (two sentences and "Delete my account" behind a confirmation that puts "Keep my account" first), Sign out. Rows are white cards with a 44 px icon, the name, the state in words and one 64 px button; the long lists of frame 08 (providers, senders, breaches) move behind "Look".

Deliver: the frames above at Normal text size, plus Today, the Check choices and Settings at Largest; one French frame (Today) to prove the layout holds; a short note per frame saying what changed and why; every button's label as a verb; the Coming soon rows visibly inert. Do not add features beyond this list, do not add a hamburger menu, do not use color as the only signal, and keep the number of words on each screen lower than today.
