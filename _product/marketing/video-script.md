# Prudence: 3-minute video script v2 (Oct 29)

Decided Oct 28: the product is **Prudence** (tagline "Ask twice"), and **Linda (US, English)** leads; her trusted person is her grandson **Thomas**, the "new number" is **Ethan**. This script follows the storyboard in [../research/how-to-win.md](../research/how-to-win.md) (Video blueprint), updated for three facts from Oct 27–28:
- The organizers require the video to **open** with one line saying what was pre-existing and what was built this week ([../../docs/PROVENANCE.md](../../docs/PROVENANCE.md)).
- The senior app is the V2 layout: "Today", one message at a time, Throw it away / Undo, Ask by text ([../specs/senior-verdict-screen.md](../specs/senior-verdict-screen.md)).
- Linda is American: USPS and Amazon, not Chronopost.
- v2 (Oct 29): the card reads verdict, headline, what to do, then the reasons; the two answers sit in a bar under the card; the speaker button says "Listen"; the type is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next; "Keep it" can be undone; a Be careful verdict gets one look at where its links land before it is shown. Filmed at the Largest text size (no overflow, EN and FR, checked Oct 29).

Narration: about 270 words, read by Hadrien at a calm pace (110–140 words a minute), recorded separately; the silent beats leave room for the app reading the card aloud. Captions burned in (assume muted viewing). Phone scenes at 390 px width or a real phone; browser at 125–150 %. Every state on screen for at least 2 s. Hard stop 2:57.

Status: **Built** = in the app today · **To build** = owner and day · **If stable** = only if it passes the Nov 1 rehearsal.
