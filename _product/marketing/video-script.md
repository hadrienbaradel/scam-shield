- v2 (Oct 29): the card reads verdict, headline, what to do, then the reasons; the two answers sit in a bar under the card; the speaker button says "Listen"; the type is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next; "Keep it" can be undone; a Be careful verdict gets one look at where its links land before it is shown.
